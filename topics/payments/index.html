Canada Gazette, Part I, Volume 160, Number 43: Stablecoin Regulations

Canada’s Department of Finance prepublished proposed Stablecoin Regulations on 24 October 2026, with related Retail Payment Activities Regulations amendments. The consultation proposes requirements for issuer registration, reserves, redemption, governance, data security, recovery and resolution, reporting and notices; representations are invited within 60 days.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-23

Gazettal of revised SPM module CA-B-2 “Systemically Important Banks”

HKMA gazetted CA-B-2 V.3 effective 23 October 2026, replacing V.2. The revised D-SIB assessment adds RTGS/FPS turnover to deposits and customer lending as substitutability indicators; all licensed banks are in the regular assessment scope and other authorised institutions may be included case by case.

SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-14

MAS Revokes the Major Payment Institution License of Sir Money Changer Pte Ltd

MAS revoked Sir Money Changer Pte. Ltd.'s Major Payment Institution Licence effective 15 September 2026 after finding that a third party independently operated a cross-border transfer business under its name and that the firm provided false or misleading information. MAS states that licensees must not allow third parties to use their licences and payment providers must maintain effective oversight and control.
