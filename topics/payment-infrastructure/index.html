DBS, OCBC and UOB complete first live tokenised-deposit transactions on Swift’s ledger

DBS, OCBC and UOB announced that they completed live domestic Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits on Swift’s blockchain-based ledger. The banks say the work provides insights for round-the-clock interbank payments and may support future corporate treasury, trade and digital-commerce services; it is not a Hong Kong regulatory requirement or a confirmed customer rollout.
