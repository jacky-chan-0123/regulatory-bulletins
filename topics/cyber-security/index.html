GC26/9: Cyber risk and resilience management

The FCA, PRA and Bank of England opened a joint consultation on non-Handbook guidance that clarifies existing UK rules. It sets draft proportionate cyber-risk and recovery expectations for specified UK firms; responses close 26 February 2027 and the source expects final guidance in H1 2027.
