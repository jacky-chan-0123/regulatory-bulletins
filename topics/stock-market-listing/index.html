Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) (Amendment) Rules 2026 and Securities and Futures Ordinance (Amendment of Schedule 8) Order 2026

The Legislative Council Brief states that the SFC made the Amendment Rules and that the Chief Executive in Council ordered the Schedule 8 amendment. The stated timetable is Gazette publication on 27 November 2026, LegCo tabling on 2 December 2026 and commencement on 1 March 2027. The changes include continuing and post-listing conditions, an information power over listed issuers and expanded reviewable decisions.
