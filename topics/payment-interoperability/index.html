Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Indonesia Strengthen Cooperation in Cross-Border Payments

HKMA and Bank Indonesia signed an MoU to explore QR-code cross-border payment interoperability and hold technical, operational and regulatory dialogues. The announcement states no implementation arrangement, participant requirement or deadline.
