HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-10-21

LCQ1: Improving online bank account opening

A Legislative Council reply reports existing HKMA expectations for remote account-opening identity verification, control enhancement, ongoing due diligence, transaction monitoring and suspicious-transaction reporting. It also reports first-phase iAM Smart step-up-authentication testing; any expansion remains subject to HKMA’s assessment of security, stability and customer experience on a risk-based approach.

LCQ17: Accelerated settlement for cash market

The Hong Kong Government says HKEX is preparing a proposed T+1 cash-market settlement cycle and targets consultation conclusions and implementation arrangements within 2026; no final implementation date or participant requirements are stated.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau · 2026-10-07

SFST articulates Hong Kong's gold master plan at Global Precious Metals Conference

FSTB reports that the trial Hong Kong Gold Central Clearing and Settlement System has 11 banks as provisional direct participants and is scheduled for formal launch in Q1 2027, without mandating bank participation or setting a final rulebook.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-09-16

The Chief Executive's 2026 Policy Address — International Financial Centre

The Policy Address says the HKMA will introduce a seven-day offshore RMB liquidity tender mechanism, explore offshore RMB short-term debt instruments, and encourage more foreign banks in Hong Kong to join CIPS directly. It is market-development direction, not a stated binding requirement or timetable for authorised institutions.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-09-16

Policy Address signals future consultation on AI-enabled fraudulent content

The Hong Kong Government says the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau will consult later in 2026 on expanding a legal framework to encourage technology and telecommunications sectors to improve detection and removal of potentially fraudulent online content, including AI-generated content. The announcement states no consultation date, final scope, implementation date or bank duty.
