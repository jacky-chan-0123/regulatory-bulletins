HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-06

Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Negara Malaysia Strengthen Bilateral Cooperation

HKMA and Bank Negara Malaysia announced bilateral workstreams on compliant cross-border stablecoin use cases, tokenisation and digital money, SME trade-finance data, cyber resilience, RTGS connectivity and possible central securities depository linkage. The announcement states collaboration and exploration, not a participation mandate, technical specification or implementation timetable for authorised institutions.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-05

HKMA hosts CargoX Solution Day to promote regional trade data connectivity

HKMA reported that CargoX Connect links the Commercial Data Interchange with a Shanghai Data Bureau digital-trade platform, enabling five pilot banks to use Yangtze River Delta trade-declaration data for trade verification and counterparty due diligence. The programme update reports ten approved trade-finance loans totalling HK$180 million and an intended extension to other Mainland locations; the release does not state a participation mandate or new control standard.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority, The Hong Kong Association of Banks · 2026-09-28

Banking industry further extends loan repayment grace period for affected individuals of Tai Po Fire

HKMA and HKAB announced that all 28 retail banks will extend by a further six months, to end-May 2027, the principal-and-interest repayment grace period for affected individuals’ existing mortgages, personal loans and credit-card loans. Wang Fuk Court mortgagee banks will continue flexible mortgage handling, including extending the original-mortgage grace period until a newly purchased unit is ready for intake.

Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Indonesia Strengthen Cooperation in Cross-Border Payments

HKMA and Bank Indonesia signed an MoU to explore QR-code cross-border payment interoperability and hold technical, operational and regulatory dialogues. The announcement states no implementation arrangement, participant requirement or deadline.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-09

Circular on Private Market Investments

HKMA reminded registered institutions distributing publicly or privately offered products with private-credit or private-equity exposure to apply product due diligence, appropriate risk ratings, clear risk disclosure, suitability controls, monitoring and staff training. The circular states no source deadline and preserves existing institutional and qualified-corporate professional-investor exemptions.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-07

HKMA launches public consultation on Phase 2B prototype of Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance

HKMA is consulting on the Phase 2B prototype of the Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance, which adds 10 economic activities and includes green, transition and climate-adaptation elements. Comments are invited by 7 October 2026.
