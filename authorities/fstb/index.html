Second Reading moved for Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2026

The Second Reading speech describes a Bill that would introduce statutory REIT restructuring and compulsory-acquisition mechanisms and clarify market-misconduct and disclosure coverage for exchange-listed collective investment schemes; implementation remains conditional on passage.

HK · Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau · 2026-11-13

Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026 gazetted

The Bill, gazetted for First Reading on 25 November 2026, proposes a second-tier MPF arrears surcharge, employer information notices, trustee sanctions and member-charge restrictions; it is not a current obligation.

HK · Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau · 2026-11-11

SFST moves Second Reading of Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2026

FSTB moved Second Reading of the Bill, which proposes REIT restructuring and compulsory-acquisition mechanisms and extends aspects of market-misconduct and disclosure regulation to listed collective investment schemes. It remains subject to passage; certain disclosure amendments are expected on 4 December 2027.

HK · Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau · 2026-11-11

LCQ14: Building international gold trading market

The government reported that the gold clearing and settlement system is in trial operation with 11 provisional direct-participant banks and is planned to launch in Q1 2027. It is to connect with RTGS for delivery-versus-payment before launch; no bank participation requirement or compliance deadline is stated.
