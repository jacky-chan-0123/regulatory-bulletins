Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-22

GS-2 Transition Planning — corrected finding

HKMA issued GS-2 V.1 on 22 September 2026 as a non-statutory SPM guidance note applicable to all authorized institutions. It sets supervisory expectations for governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets, stakeholder engagement, scenario analysis and communication.
