HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority, The Hong Kong Association of Banks · 2026-09-28

Banking industry further extends loan repayment grace period for affected individuals of Tai Po Fire

HKMA and HKAB announced that all 28 retail banks will extend by a further six months, to end-May 2027, the principal-and-interest repayment grace period for affected individuals’ existing mortgages, personal loans and credit-card loans. Wang Fuk Court mortgagee banks will continue flexible mortgage handling, including extending the original-mortgage grace period until a newly purchased unit is ready for intake.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission, Accounting and Financial Reporting Council · 2026-09-28

SFC and AFRC extend regulatory cooperation to financial reporting of licensed firms and funds under new MoU

The SFC and AFRC signed a new MoU on 28 September 2026, replacing their February 2021 MoU. It covers financial and, where applicable, compliance reporting by licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual asset service providers, SFC-authorised funds and registered open-ended fund companies, and related audit and assurance work. The MoU provides for information sharing, referrals, mutual assistance and coordinated inspections or investigations in matters of mutual regulatory interest, subject to law.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-23

Review of the Enhanced Competency Framework for Banking Practitioners

HKMA proposes a revised Enhanced Competency Framework for all AIs, including a common Core Level, ten Professional-Level workstreams, revised experience requirements and integrated qualifications. It advises uninterrupted current ECF training while guidance and transition arrangements are developed; milestones are indicative rather than a fixed compliance date.
