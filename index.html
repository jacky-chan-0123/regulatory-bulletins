HK · Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited · 2026-10-16

Implementation of T+1 Settlement Cycle for Hong Kong Cash Market

HKSCC states that, subject to market readiness and regulatory approval, Hong Kong cash-market settlement will move from T+2 to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The circular asks Clearing Participants to assess T-day post-trade operations, liquidity and funding, client cut-offs and communications, customer documents, and exception controls.

SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-14

MAS Revokes the Major Payment Institution License of Sir Money Changer Pte Ltd

MAS revoked Sir Money Changer Pte. Ltd.'s Major Payment Institution Licence effective 15 September 2026 after finding that a third party independently operated a cross-border transfer business under its name and that the firm provided false or misleading information. MAS states that licensees must not allow third parties to use their licences and payment providers must maintain effective oversight and control.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-10-13

Motor finance legal challenge to be heard in February 2027

The FCA says challenges to its motor-finance compensation scheme will be heard from 16 to 26 February 2027. While the scheme is partly suspended, lenders must continue specified preparation and complaint activity; the FCA expects rigorous contingency planning, financial provisions and auditor engagement if the scheme or parts of it are quashed.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-15

Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence

HKMA issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions. Annex A supersedes the 2019 AI principles and expects proportionate governance across the AI lifecycle, including ownership, inventory, risk-based testing and monitoring, recovery, records, data and third-party safeguards, and fair and transparent customer use. HKMA states that policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner; no fixed deadline is stated. Annex B provides non-exhaustive Sandbox insights, while Annex C is preliminary and does not establish current agentic-AI requirements.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-14

Chongqing–Hong Kong Cross-boundary Data Validation Platform

HKMA announced the platform's launch for initial cross-boundary financial use cases, including account opening and loan applications. It verifies customer-presented data using blockchain technology and hash values without cross-boundary transfer or storage of original documents; the announcement states no participation requirement or timetable.
