Hong Kong · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-26

SFC approves first-ever secondary trading of tokenised public funds

The SFC approved secondary trading of two existing SFC-authorised tokenised money-market funds on an SFC-licensed virtual-asset trading platform, with around-the-clock trading beginning on 26 October 2026. The approval applies the April 2026 pilot framework; it is not a new sector-wide rule or direct instruction outside the approved arrangement.
