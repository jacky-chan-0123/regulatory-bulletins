HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-06

Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Negara Malaysia Strengthen Bilateral Cooperation

HKMA and Bank Negara Malaysia announced bilateral workstreams on compliant cross-border stablecoin use cases, tokenisation and digital money, SME trade-finance data, cyber resilience, RTGS connectivity and possible central securities depository linkage. The announcement states collaboration and exploration, not a participation mandate, technical specification or implementation timetable for authorised institutions.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-05

HKMA hosts CargoX Solution Day to promote regional trade data connectivity

HKMA reported that CargoX Connect links the Commercial Data Interchange with a Shanghai Data Bureau digital-trade platform, enabling five pilot banks to use Yangtze River Delta trade-declaration data for trade verification and counterparty due diligence. The programme update reports ten approved trade-finance loans totalling HK$180 million and an intended extension to other Mainland locations; the release does not state a participation mandate or new control standard.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-29

SFC reprimands and fines Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited $7 million and suspends its responsible officer for regulatory breaches

SFC reprimanded and fined Zheng Da HK$7 million for AML/CFT and other failures, and suspended a responsible officer from 28 September 2026 to 27 April 2027. The final disciplinary action concerns customer-supplied futures-order systems, deposit enquiries and suspicious-trading monitoring.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-09-30

FCA opens the gateway to regulated crypto

FCA opened applications for UK crypto-firm authorisation. Firms intending to continue operating in the UK should apply by 28 February 2027, ahead of the new regime taking effect on 25 October 2027.

HK · The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited · 2026-09-30

HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

HSBC says its forthcoming Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin will be called HSBC RedCoin and is planned for a second-half 2026 launch. It says launch availability will initially be through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App for P2P and P2M payments.
