Anti-bribery & corruption controls for account opening

HKMA issued a circular to all authorised institutions on account-opening bribery risks. It expects layered, risk-based onboarding and proportionate customer due diligence, with segregation of duties and enhanced controls for third-party introducer cases; no source deadline is stated.

HSBC RedCoin launches today

HSBC says its HKD stablecoin, RedCoin (RCHKD), will be available from launch on PayMe. In the coming days, the bank says more than 3.3 million PayMe users will be able to activate an in-app wallet and use it for peer-to-peer and peer-to-merchant payments, including at more than 100,000 merchants; it also describes later planned mobile-app, tokenised-investment and wholesale use cases.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-22

Anti-bribery & corruption controls for account opening

HKMA issued a current circular to all Authorized Institutions on bribery and corruption risks in account opening. It expects a layered, risk-based approach and proportionate customer due diligence; segregation of duties and enhanced controls for third-party agents are explicit requirements. No source deadline is stated.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-10-21

LCQ1: Improving online bank account opening

A Legislative Council reply reports existing HKMA expectations for remote account-opening identity verification, control enhancement, ongoing due diligence, transaction monitoring and suspicious-transaction reporting. It also reports first-phase iAM Smart step-up-authentication testing; any expansion remains subject to HKMA’s assessment of security, stability and customer experience on a risk-based approach.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-10-19

Building the future of finance in the age of AI

In a drafted speech, the Financial Conduct Authority shared AI good and poor practice drawn from firm testing, covering customer escalation, governance, testing, monitoring, resilience and supplier dependencies. The FCA said significant AI failures and near misses should be covered by UK major-incident reporting and said it will develop an agentic-finance framework. This is a UK speech and an analogous control benchmark, not a Hong Kong obligation.
