HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-30

Roadmap for Implementing the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the Exchange-traded Derivatives Market

The SFC roadmap schedules HKIDR-DM implementation for Q2 2028, subject to market readiness, testing and rehearsals, and sets preparation milestones for relevant licensed corporations and registered institutions. The proposed Code provision remains to be gazetted and will take effect on a future SFC-determined date.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-30

Obtaining client consent under the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the exchange-traded derivatives market (HKIDR-DM)

The SFC says relevant intermediaries will need express consent from individual clients for specified CID and BCAN collection and transfer under HKIDR-DM. It sets out identity-authentication, record and log expectations, while the proposed Code provision remains to be gazetted and its effective date is to be determined by the SFC.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-30

Participating banks in IP Financing Sandbox complete credit approval for first batch of pilot cases

HKMA reports that three participating banks completed credit approvals for seven IP Financing Sandbox pilot cases, using independent IP valuation reports in credit assessment. HKMA describes the Sandbox as a risk-controlled pilot and does not state a broader participation requirement or a new supervisory deadline.

HK · HSBC · 2026-09-30

HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

HSBC announced the name of its planned Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin and says it plans a phased H2 2026 launch, initially through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App for P2P and P2M payments. The bank statement does not confirm a launch date, licence terms, corporate availability, redemption design or cross-border scope.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority, The Hong Kong Association of Banks · 2026-09-28

Banking industry further extends loan repayment grace period for affected individuals of Tai Po Fire

HKMA and HKAB announced that all 28 retail banks will extend by a further six months, to end-May 2027, the principal-and-interest repayment grace period for affected individuals’ existing mortgages, personal loans and credit-card loans. Wang Fuk Court mortgagee banks will continue flexible mortgage handling, including extending the original-mortgage grace period until a newly purchased unit is ready for intake.
