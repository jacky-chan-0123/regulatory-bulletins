Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) (Amendment) Rules 2026 and Securities and Futures Ordinance (Amendment of Schedule 8) Order 2026

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-11-25

Launch of CMU Digital Asset Platform

HKMA announced the launch of the CMU Digital Asset Platform, operated by CMU OmniClear on its behalf. The platform supports debt-securities issuance, clearing and settlement, coupon payments, redemption and tokenisation, with atomic on-chain delivery-versus-payment and interfaces to RTGS systems and central bank digital currencies. The source describes future exploration of additional asset classes and payment instruments.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-11-23

SFC issues restriction notice to DA YUE Financial Group Limited

On 23 November 2026, the SFC issued DA YUE Financial Group Limited a restriction notice under sections 204 and 205 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Without prior written consent, the firm cannot conduct its licensed activities or deal with relevant property, subject to stated exceptions. The SFC says its initial enquiries indicate an approximately HK$29.6 million client-money shortfall and its review is ongoing.
