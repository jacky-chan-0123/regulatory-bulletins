Singapore · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-07

MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines — operative document verified

MAS's final Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management apply to all financial institutions and all forms of AI as supervisory expectations. The reviewed operative document sets enterprise and use-case governance, board and senior-management accountability, inventories and materiality assessment, and proportionate lifecycle controls. Paragraphs 5.10–5.11 require risk-materiality-appropriate third-party AI controls and state that the FI retains primary accountability; where residual risk cannot be brought within risk appetite, the FI should consider limiting or suspending the service or replacing the provider. Paragraph 2.3 permits basic governance where poor performance or unavailability is unlikely to have a material adverse impact. Paragraph 1.8 sets 7 October 2027 for Sections 3–4 and 7 October 2028 for Sections 5–6. The official media release remains supporting evidence; the 30-page Guidelines are now the primary operative source.

HKMA issued a Practice Guide on Data Excellence to all Authorized Institutions.
