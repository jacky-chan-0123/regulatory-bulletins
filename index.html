HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-09-16

The Chief Executive's 2026 Policy Address — International Financial Centre

The Policy Address says the HKMA will introduce a seven-day offshore RMB liquidity tender mechanism, explore offshore RMB short-term debt instruments, and encourage more foreign banks in Hong Kong to join CIPS directly. It is market-development direction, not a stated binding requirement or timetable for authorised institutions.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-09-16

Crypto firms get guidance on how the new regime applies

The FCA says its future UK cryptoasset-regime guidance explains which activities may require authorisation, including qualifying-stablecoin issuance, platform operation, dealing or arranging, safeguarding and arranging staking. Applications open on 30 September 2026 and the regime is due to enter force on 25 October 2027; this is a UK benchmark, not a Hong Kong requirement.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-09-16

Policy Address signals future consultation on AI-enabled fraudulent content

The Hong Kong Government says the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau will consult later in 2026 on expanding a legal framework to encourage technology and telecommunications sectors to improve detection and removal of potentially fraudulent online content, including AI-generated content. The announcement states no consultation date, final scope, implementation date or bank duty.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-11

SFC circular on Haiti and Yemen sanctions amendments

The SFC reported that the Haiti and Yemen sanctions amendment regulations were gazetted on 11 September 2026 with immediate effect. The underlying regulations are required within their legal scope; the circular addresses licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities and does not state a new obligation for authorised institutions generally.
