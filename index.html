SFC issues restriction notice to DA YUE Financial Group Limited

On 23 November 2026, the SFC issued DA YUE Financial Group Limited a restriction notice under sections 204 and 205 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Without prior written consent, the firm cannot conduct its licensed activities or deal with relevant property, subject to stated exceptions. The SFC says its initial enquiries indicate an approximately HK$29.6 million client-money shortfall and its review is ongoing.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-11-19

Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks

HKMA expects all authorised institutions to review the HKAB Guideline on Corruption Prevention for Banks, assess current policies and procedures, plan necessary enhancements where appropriate, and report compliance status by end-Q3 2027. The Guideline consolidates existing requirements and practical anti-corruption measures; the circular is a supervisory expectation, not a new statutory rule or prescribed uniform control design.

UK financial watchdogs clarify cyber expectations as attacks rise

The FCA, PRA and Bank of England propose non-rigid guidance that consolidates existing UK requirements and supervisory lessons on cyber risk and resilience. Responses to GC26/9 close on 26 February 2027 and final guidance is expected in H1 2027. This is a UK consultation and comparable benchmark, not a Hong Kong obligation.
