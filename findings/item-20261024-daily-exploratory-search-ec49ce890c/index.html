Canada’s Department of Finance prepublished proposed Stablecoin Regulations on 24 October 2026, alongside related amendments to the Retail Payment Activities Regulations. The consultation proposes details on issuer registration, one-to-one reserves, redemption, governance, data security, recovery and resolution, reporting and notices, and would bring specified stablecoin payment functions by PSPs into the Canadian framework. Representations are invited within 60 days of publication, and the stated intention is commencement with the Act in fall 2027. This is a Canadian consultation rather than final law or a Hong Kong requirement; it may affect any Canada-facing stablecoin, wallet, custody, PSP or provider operating model, subject to the final scope and exclusions.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-23

Gazettal of revised SPM module CA-B-2 “Systemically Important Banks”

HKMA gazetted CA-B-2 V.3 effective 23 October 2026, replacing V.2. The revised D-SIB assessment adds RTGS/FPS turnover to deposits and customer lending as substitutability indicators; all licensed banks are in the regular assessment scope and other authorised institutions may be included case by case.
