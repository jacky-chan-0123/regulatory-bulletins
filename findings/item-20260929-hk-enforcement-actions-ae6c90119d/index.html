SFC reprimands and fines Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited $7 million and suspends its responsible officer for regulatory breaches

SFC reprimanded and fined Zheng Da HK$7 million for AML/CFT and other failures, and suspended a responsible officer from 28 September 2026 to 27 April 2027. The final disciplinary action concerns customer-supplied futures-order systems, deposit enquiries and suspicious-trading monitoring.

⏰ Deadline: Responsible-officer suspension ends 27 April 2027; no broader source deadline stated
Products: Futures trading; securities onboarding; order systems

SFC reprimanded and fined Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited HK$7 million for AML/CFT and other failures during 1 December 2021 to 30 September 2023, and suspended responsible officer Zhong Hao from 28 September 2026 to 27 April 2027. SFC found inadequate due diligence and testing of customer-supplied futures-order systems, unsupported deposit enquiries in eight client accounts, and ineffective monitoring of suspicious trading; Zheng Da disabled the systems from August 2025. This final, case-specific disciplinary action concerns an SFC Type 2/5 intermediary and existing AML/CFT, AML Guideline and Code obligations, not a new HKMA requirement; comparable securities businesses may test system approval, surveillance and escalation evidence.
