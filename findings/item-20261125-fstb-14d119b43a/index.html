Legislative Council Brief and annexed subsidiary legislation · 2026-11-25

Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) amendments

Hong Kong subsidiary legislation scheduled to commence on 1 March 2027 permits continuing and post-listing conditions and issuer information requisitions. It is required for parties within its statutory perimeter; the source does not establish a direct bank-wide obligation.

⏰ Deadline: 1 March 2027 (commencement)
Products: Investment, capital-markets services

The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau and Securities and Futures Commission brief states that the Amendment Rules were made under section 36(1) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, with the related Schedule 8 Order made on 17 November 2026. The instruments are scheduled for Gazette publication on 27 November 2026, Legislative Council tabling on 2 December 2026 and commencement on 1 March 2027. From commencement, the changes permit continuing listing-application conditions, post-listing conditions and issuer information requisitions; non-compliance with a post-listing condition may lead to suspension. The amendments are required for parties within their statutory perimeter, while the source does not establish a direct bank-wide obligation; listed issuer entities and securities businesses supporting affected listings may need condition tracking, issuer-disclosure, escalation and response arrangements.

Monitoring: Confirm which entities, transactions and service roles are in scope, and whether the Gazette text or Legislative Council scrutiny changes the stated commencement or transition provisions.

Source: Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) (Amendment) Rules 2026 and Securities and Futures Ordinance (Amendment of Schedule 8) Order 2026

Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) (Amendment) Rules 2026 and Securities and Futures Ordinance (Amendment of Schedule 8) Order 2026

The Legislative Council Brief states that the SFC made the Amendment Rules and that the Chief Executive in Council ordered the Schedule 8 amendment. On 23 November 2026, the SFC issued DA YUE Financial Group Limited a restriction notice under sections 204 and 205 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.
