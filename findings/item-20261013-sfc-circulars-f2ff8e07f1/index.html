SFC supervisory circular · 2026-10-13

Circular to licensed corporations carrying out sponsor work and engaging in bookbuilding and placing activities

The SFC issued a supervisory circular for scoped sponsor, overall-coordinator and capital-market-intermediary roles, highlighting diligence, escalation, reporting and rejection of unresolved suspicious orders in IPO activity.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: IPO advisory, Bookbuilding, Placing

On 13 October, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) issued a circular following its January communication on sponsor, bookbuilding and placing activity. It reported fewer strained sponsors and Principals, but identified uneven Principal submissions and dubious IPO arrangements. The SFC expects sponsors, overall coordinators and capital-market intermediaries to investigate, document, escalate and report concerns, and states that overall coordinators and capital-market intermediaries shall reject unresolved suspicious orders. This is a supervisory communication for the specified SFC-regulated roles, supported by underlying Code of Conduct and listing-rule duties rather than a universal banking duty. Scoped businesses may need to evidence Principal capacity, investor and funding enquiries, order-book records, escalation, reporting and rejection decisions.
