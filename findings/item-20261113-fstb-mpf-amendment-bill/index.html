Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026 gazetted

The Bill, gazetted for First Reading on 25 November 2026, proposes a second-tier MPF arrears surcharge, employer information notices, trustee sanctions and member-charge restrictions; it is not a current obligation.

⏰ Deadline: 25 November 2026 — First Reading, not a compliance deadline
Products: MPF, retirement investment

The Government gazetted the Bill on 13 November for First Reading on 25 November. It proposes a second-tier 10% surcharge where MPF arrears, including the existing 5% surcharge, remain unpaid 120 days after contribution day, alongside MPFA information notices with at least 14 days to respond. It also proposes trustee sanctions of up to HK$10 million or three times profit gained or loss avoided, whichever is greater, public reprimand, a prohibition on passing trustee penalties to schemes, funds or members, and intermediary discipline and investment/risk-management measures. The proposals are not current obligations; MPF trustees, intermediaries and group employers should assess role applicability and preserve a conditional mapping of arrears, notice, governance and member-charge processes pending final legislation and MPFA guidance.
