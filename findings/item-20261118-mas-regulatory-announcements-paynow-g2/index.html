MAS and ABS Set Out PayNow Generation 2 Implementation Roadmap

MAS and ABS published a Singapore PayNow Gen2 roadmap covering staged enhancements to QR interoperability, deep-linking, selected higher-value government payments, person-to-merchant dispute arrangements, richer payment data and micropayment testing. The roadmap targets foundational standards and trials by end-2027, with staged expansion in 2028; it is a coordinated implementation roadmap rather than an immediate MAS rule.

⏰ Deadline: Singapore roadmap: end-2027 foundational standards and trials; staged 2028 rollouts
Products: Payments, Digital, Merchant payments

MAS and the Association of Banks in Singapore published a PayNow Gen2 implementation roadmap for Singapore. The roadmap covers seven staged enhancements, including SGQR+ interoperability, deep-linking, selected higher-value government payments, person-to-merchant dispute, fraud and scam arrangements, richer payment data and micropayment testing. Common deep-linking standards and a central facility are targeted by end-2027, with limited SGQR+ and higher-value-payment trials planned in 2027 and staged expansion in 2028. For comparable Hong Kong payment providers, the roadmap may inform monitoring of deep-link security, QR routing, dispute evidence and data, reconciliation, and cross-border interoperability; it is not an immediate MAS rule and does not establish Hong Kong requirements.

Singapore · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-07

MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines — operative document verified

MAS's final Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management apply to all financial institutions and all forms of AI as supervisory expectations. The reviewed operative document sets enterprise and use-case governance, board and senior-management accountability, inventories and materiality assessment, and proportionate lifecycle controls. Paragraphs 5.10–5.11 require risk-materiality-appropriate third-party AI controls and state that the FI retains primary accountability; where residual risk cannot be brought within risk appetite, the FI should consider limiting or suspending the service or replacing the provider. Paragraph 2.3 permits basic governance where poor performance or unavailability is unlikely to have a material adverse impact. Paragraph 1.8 sets 7 October 2027 for Sections 3–4 and 7 October 2028 for Sections 5–6. The official media release remains supporting evidence; the 30-page Guidelines are now the primary operative source.
