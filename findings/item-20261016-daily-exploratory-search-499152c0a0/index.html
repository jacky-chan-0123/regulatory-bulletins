HKSCC participant circular · 2026-10-16

Implementation of T+1 Settlement Cycle for Hong Kong Cash Market

HKSCC states that, subject to market readiness and regulatory approval, Hong Kong cash-market settlement will move from T+2 to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The circular asks Clearing Participants to assess T-day post-trade operations, liquidity and funding, client cut-offs and communications, customer documents, and exception controls.

⏰ Deadline: Second half of 2028 (target implementation; subject to market readiness and regulatory approval)
Products: Securities investment, custody, settlement

HKSCC states that, subject to market readiness and regulatory approval, Hong Kong cash-market settlement will move from T+2 to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The circular is addressed to HKSCC Clearing Participants and says the shorter cycle means many post-trade activities must be completed on T-day. HKSCC expects Clearing Participants to assess systems and operations, liquidity, cash funding and stock availability, client cut-offs and communications, customer documents, and exception-management controls. Banks acting as brokers, custodians or settlement agents may be affected through post-trade, liquidity, customer-service, data and vendor dependencies; the circular does not establish an HKMA requirement or a fixed commencement date.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-12

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The uncertificated securities market regime takes effect on 16 November 2026. The SFC approved six securities registrars effective that date; listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar, and existing prescribed securities constituted under Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Mainland China or Hong Kong law have five years to participate.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-12

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The SFC approved six securities registrars effective 16 November 2026, when the USM regime takes effect. Listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar; the stated requirements apply to issuers and registrars, not every bank.
