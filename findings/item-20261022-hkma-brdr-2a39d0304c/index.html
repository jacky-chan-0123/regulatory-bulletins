Gazettal of revised SPM module CA-B-2 “Systemically Important Banks”

HKMA gazetted CA-B-2 V.3 effective 23 October 2026, replacing V.2. The revised D-SIB assessment adds RTGS/FPS turnover to deposits and customer lending as substitutability indicators; all licensed banks are in the regular assessment scope and other authorised institutions may be included case by case.

⏰ Deadline: Effective 23 October 2026; 12 months from HKMA notification for a newly or increased HLA requirement
Products: Deposits, payments, lending

HKMA gazetted CA-B-2 V.3 on 23 October 2026, effective that day, replacing V.2. The statutory guideline adds RTGS/FPS turnover to deposits and customer lending as substitutability indicators in the domestic systemically important bank assessment. All licensed banks are within the regular assessment scope, while other authorised institutions may be included case by case; institutions in scope submit MA(BS)24 data. A designated D-SIB, or one with a higher HLA requirement, has 12 months from formal HKMA notification to meet that requirement. The change may affect payment-data lineage, prudential reporting, capital planning and recovery or resolution management information; the source does not announce a designation, HLA amount or reporting date for any institution.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-15

Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence

HKMA issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions. Annex A supersedes the 2019 AI principles and expects proportionate governance across the AI lifecycle, including ownership, inventory, risk-based testing and monitoring, recovery, records, data and third-party safeguards, and fair and transparent customer use. HKMA states that policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner; no fixed deadline is stated. Annex B provides non-exhaustive Sandbox insights, while Annex C is preliminary and does not establish current agentic-AI requirements.
