SFST articulates Hong Kong's gold master plan at Global Precious Metals Conference

FSTB reports that the trial Hong Kong Gold Central Clearing and Settlement System has 11 banks as provisional direct participants and is scheduled for formal launch in Q1 2027, without mandating bank participation or setting a final rulebook.

⏰ Deadline: Formal launch of the Gold Central Clearing and Settlement System is scheduled for Q1 2027
Products: Bullion settlement, RTGS, cross-border markets

FSTB reports that the trial Hong Kong Gold Central Clearing and Settlement System has 11 banks as provisional direct participants, with formal launch scheduled for Q1 2027. Connection to Hong Kong RTGS for delivery-versus-payment settlement is in its final stages. The Government and the London Bullion Market Association are exploring delivery-standards co-operation, contingent on materialisation, and tax concessions are being explored. This is a policy and market-infrastructure update, not a mandatory participation requirement or final rulebook; provisional or prospective participants may need to consider clearing, RTGS, custody and settlement-risk dependencies.
