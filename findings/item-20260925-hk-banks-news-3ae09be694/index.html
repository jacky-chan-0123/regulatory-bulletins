DBS Hong Kong’s Gen AI-enabled virtual assistants reach over 1 million customers and go agentic

DBS Hong Kong says it will progressively introduce agentic capabilities for DBS IDEAL corporate and SME users from 26 September 2026. DBS says authenticated customers can complete supported banking tasks through conversation; the announcement does not state the underlying control design.

⏰ Deadline: Progressive Hong Kong introduction from 26 September 2026; later retail agentic phase planned for 2027
Products: Corporate digital banking, SME servicing, Payments

DBS Hong Kong says it will progressively introduce agentic capabilities to DBS Joy for DBS IDEAL corporate and SME users from 26 September 2026. DBS says authenticated customers can complete supported banking tasks through conversation and that the assistant retrieves, analyses and presents transaction and account information. The release is an official product announcement, not a regulatory requirement or a finding of a control failure. Comparable digital servicing may warrant a market watch on task permissions, customer authorisation, account-data use, human escalation and operational support, while the release does not establish the underlying control design.

The underlying regulations are required within their legal scope; the circular addresses licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities and does not state a new obligation for authorised institutions generally.

DBS, OCBC and UOB complete first live tokenised-deposit transactions on Swift’s ledger

DBS, OCBC and UOB announced that they completed live domestic Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits on Swift’s blockchain-based ledger. The banks say the work provides insights for round-the-clock interbank payments and may support future corporate treasury, trade and digital-commerce services; it is not a Hong Kong regulatory requirement or a confirmed customer rollout.
