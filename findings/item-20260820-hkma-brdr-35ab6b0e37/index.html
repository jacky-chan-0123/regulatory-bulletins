Implementation guidance on Banking (Capital) Rules

HKMA listed implementation guidance on the Banking (Capital) Rules for all authorized institutions, but the available evidence did not support a conclusion about changed calculations, thresholds, reporting or transition dates.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: lending, capital reporting

HKMA’s BRDR lists a current circular, issued on 20 August 2026, titled “Implementation guidance on Banking (Capital) Rules” and addressed to all authorized institutions. The listing identifies non-securitisation credit-risk capital adequacy and credit-risk management, with annexes headed Chapter III (STC approach) and Chapter IV (IRB approach). The available evidence supports a prudential applicability review, but not a conclusion on a changed capital treatment, threshold, transition, reporting date or specific obligation because the circular and annex bodies were not extracted. Prudential reporting and credit-risk owners should obtain the authenticated documents and confirm their status, applicability and any dates before mapping any material effects on capital calculations, lending economics, credit data/models, policy or returns.
