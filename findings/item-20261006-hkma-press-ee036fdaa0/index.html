Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Negara Malaysia Strengthen Bilateral Cooperation

HKMA and Bank Negara Malaysia announced bilateral workstreams on compliant cross-border stablecoin use cases, tokenisation and digital money, SME trade-finance data, cyber resilience, RTGS connectivity and possible central securities depository linkage. The announcement states collaboration and exploration, not a participation mandate, technical specification or implementation timetable for authorised institutions.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Cross-border payments, SME trade finance, digital assets

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) and Bank Negara Malaysia announced bilateral collaboration after their 2 October meeting, including a policy-focused workstream on compliant cross-border stablecoin use cases. The announced areas also cover policy and regulatory exchange on tokenisation and digital money, knowledge sharing on CDI and CargoX to expand SME trade-finance access, cyber-resilience and anti-scam cooperation, RTGS connectivity, and exploration of a CMU–RENTAS central securities depository linkage. This is a bilateral cooperation announcement rather than a rule or supervisory expectation for authorised institutions, and the release states no participation mandate, technical specification, customer or data requirement, or implementation date. Banks active in the stated Hong Kong–Malaysia activities may need to monitor whether the workstreams produce pilots, standards or participation conditions that affect interoperability, customer/data flows, fraud and cyber controls, or partner arrangements.
