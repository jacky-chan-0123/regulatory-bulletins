SFC press release and consultation conclusions · 2026-10-12

SFC proceeds to strengthen targeted tools to tackle IPO-related and other corporate misconduct

The SFC will proceed with proposed Stock Market Listing Rules and related Securities and Futures Ordinance amendments, with expected commencement in the first quarter of 2027 subject to the legislative process. This is an announced rule-change process, not a current final obligation.

⏰ Deadline: Q1 2027 expected commencement, subject to legislative process
Products: Investment, IPO advisory

Following consultation, the SFC will proceed with clarified and refined amendments to the Stock Market Listing Rules and related Securities and Futures Ordinance provisions. The proposed changes include continuing conditions on listing applicants and post-listing conditions as alternatives to immediate suspension, streamlined resumption procedures, and a merits-review mechanism through the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal. The SFC expects commencement in the first quarter of 2027, subject to gazetting, negative vetting and the legislative process. This is an announced rule-change process rather than a current final obligation; SFC-registered corporate-finance businesses and group entities supporting listings may need to monitor final provisions and assess case-governance, client-advice and escalation implications.

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The uncertificated securities market regime takes effect on 16 November 2026. The SFC approved six securities registrars effective that date; listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar, and existing prescribed securities constituted under Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Mainland China or Hong Kong law have five years to participate.

SFC, AFRC and the Exchange join forces to address disclaimers of opinion solely relating to going concern

The SFC, AFRC and the Exchange issued a joint statement setting supervisory expectations for listed issuers with audit disclaimers solely relating to going concern. It expects robust assessments supported by reasonable assumptions, reliable data and feasible action plans, alongside detailed annual and quarterly progress disclosures and critical audit-committee and auditor review. The statement does not amend Listing Rules or set a new deadline; the Exchange may consider rule changes if improvement is not significant.
