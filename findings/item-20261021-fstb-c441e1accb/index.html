Legislative Council question-and-reply / government statement · 2026-10-21

LCQ1: Improving online bank account opening

A Legislative Council reply reports existing HKMA expectations for remote account-opening identity verification, control enhancement, ongoing due diligence, transaction monitoring and suspicious-transaction reporting. It also reports first-phase iAM Smart step-up-authentication testing; any expansion remains subject to HKMA’s assessment of security, stability and customer experience on a risk-based approach.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Digital onboarding, SME onboarding

The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau’s Legislative Council reply reports that HKMA already expects appropriate identity-verification and risk-management measures for remote account opening, with systems and controls continuously enhanced. It says banks must effectively identify and verify customers and, after account opening, conduct ongoing due diligence and transaction monitoring, reporting suspicious transactions to law-enforcement agencies. The reply cites 2023 guidance to use clear on-screen messages explaining that selfies or liveness tests are for account opening, and describes in-app authentication and facial recognition for mobile-device binding as existing HKMA-required measures. First-phase testing of iAM Smart step-up authentication for remote account opening has commenced; HKMA will assess security, stability and customer experience before any risk-based expansion, so the timing and scope remain open.
