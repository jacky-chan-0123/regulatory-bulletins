Record year for investment product sales in Hong Kong with robust demand for FICC-related products: SFC-HKMA joint survey 2025

The HKMA and SFC reported that 2025 non-exchange-traded investment-product sales reached HK$9.9 trillion, up 63% year on year; online sales were 21% of aggregate transaction value and currency-linked product sales were HK$698 billion. The release reports survey results and states no new deadline or requirement.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Investment products, FX-linked products, digital distribution

The HKMA and SFC published their 2025 joint-survey results on 8 September, reporting HK$9.9 trillion of non-exchange-traded investment-product sales, up 63% year on year, across more than 1.6 million transacting clients and 452 selling firms. Collective investment schemes represented 42% of sales, structured products 40% and debt securities 9%; currency-linked product sales rose 50% to HK$698 billion. Online sales accounted for 21% of aggregate transaction value, compared with 17% in 2024, and 122 respondent firms distributed investment products online. This is official market intelligence, not a new requirement or supervisory action, but it may affect product-governance, distribution-risk and capacity prioritisation where a business serves the survey’s covered client segments.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission, Accounting and Financial Reporting Council, The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited · 2026-10-08

SFC, AFRC and the Exchange join forces to address disclaimers of opinion solely relating to going concern

The SFC, AFRC and the Exchange issued a joint statement setting supervisory expectations for listed issuers with audit disclaimers solely relating to going concern. It expects robust assessments supported by reasonable assumptions, reliable data and feasible action plans, alongside detailed annual and quarterly progress disclosures and critical audit-committee and auditor review. The statement does not amend Listing Rules or set a new deadline; the Exchange may consider rule changes if improvement is not significant.

SFC and AFRC extend regulatory cooperation to financial reporting of licensed firms and funds under new MoU

The SFC and AFRC signed a new MoU on 28 September 2026, replacing their February 2021 MoU. It covers financial and, where applicable, compliance reporting by licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual asset service providers, SFC-authorised funds and registered open-ended fund companies, and related audit and assurance work. The MoU provides for information sharing, referrals, mutual assistance and coordinated inspections or investigations in matters of mutual regulatory interest, subject to law.
