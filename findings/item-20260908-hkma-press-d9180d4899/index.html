Record year for investment product sales in Hong Kong with robust demand for FICC-related products: SFC-HKMA joint survey 2025

The HKMA and SFC reported that 2025 non-exchange-traded investment-product sales reached HK$9.9 trillion, up 63% year on year; online sales were 21% of aggregate transaction value and currency-linked product sales were HK$698 billion. The release reports survey results and states no new deadline or requirement.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Investment products, FX-linked products, digital distribution

The HKMA and SFC published their 2025 joint-survey results on 8 September, reporting HK$9.9 trillion of non-exchange-traded investment-product sales, up 63% year on year, across more than 1.6 million transacting clients and 452 selling firms. Collective investment schemes represented 42% of sales, structured products 40% and debt securities 9%; currency-linked product sales rose 50% to HK$698 billion. Online sales accounted for 21% of aggregate transaction value, compared with 17% in 2024, and 122 respondent firms distributed investment products online. This is official market intelligence, not a new requirement or supervisory action, but it may affect product-governance, distribution-risk and capacity prioritisation where a business serves the survey’s covered client segments.
