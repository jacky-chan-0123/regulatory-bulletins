Official bank market announcement · 2026-09-30

HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

HSBC announced the name of its planned Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin and says it plans a phased H2 2026 launch, initially through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App for P2P and P2M payments. The bank statement does not confirm a launch date, licence terms, corporate availability, redemption design or cross-border scope.

⏰ Deadline: H2 2026 planned launch (bank statement; no confirmed date)
Products: Payments, digital assets, mobile app

HSBC says its forthcoming Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin will be called HSBC RedCoin and that it plans a second-half 2026 launch under a licence it says was granted in April. At launch, it says availability will be limited to PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App, initially for P2P and P2M payments before prospective wholesale corporate and institutional use cases. It also says it will publish scam-prevention and redemption education. This is a bank market announcement and commercial development, not an HKMA or SFC instrument: it does not verify licence terms, a launch date, corporate availability, redemption design, reserve arrangements or cross-border scope.
