Official bank market announcement · 2026-09-30

HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

HSBC announced the name of its planned Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin and says it plans a phased H2 2026 launch, initially through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App for P2P and P2M payments. The bank statement does not confirm a launch date, licence terms, corporate availability, redemption design or cross-border scope.

⏰ Deadline: H2 2026 planned launch (bank statement; no confirmed date)
Products: Payments, digital assets, mobile app

HSBC says its forthcoming Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin will be called HSBC RedCoin and that it plans a second-half 2026 launch under a licence it says was granted in April. At launch, it says availability will be limited to PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App, initially for P2P and P2M payments before prospective wholesale corporate and institutional use cases. It also says it will publish scam-prevention and redemption education. This is a bank market announcement and commercial development, not an HKMA or SFC instrument: it does not verify licence terms, a launch date, corporate availability, redemption design, reserve arrangements or cross-border scope.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-23

Gazettal of revised SPM module CA-B-2 “Systemically Important Banks”

HKMA gazetted CA-B-2 V.3 effective 23 October 2026, replacing V.2. The revised D-SIB assessment adds RTGS/FPS turnover to deposits and customer lending as substitutability indicators; all licensed banks are in the regular assessment scope and other authorised institutions may be included case by case.

HSBC RedCoin launches today

HSBC says its HKD stablecoin, RedCoin (RCHKD), will be available from launch on PayMe. In the coming days, the bank says more than 3.3 million PayMe users will be able to activate an in-app wallet and use it for peer-to-peer and peer-to-merchant payments, including at more than 100,000 merchants; it also describes later planned mobile-app, tokenised-investment and wholesale use cases.

SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-14

MAS Revokes the Major Payment Institution License of Sir Money Changer Pte Ltd

MAS revoked Sir Money Changer Pte. Ltd.'s Major Payment Institution Licence effective 15 September 2026 after finding that a third party independently operated a cross-border transfer business under its name and that the firm provided false or misleading information. MAS states that licensees must not allow third parties to use their licences and payment providers must maintain effective oversight and control.
