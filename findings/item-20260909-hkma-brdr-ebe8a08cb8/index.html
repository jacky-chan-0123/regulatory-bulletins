HKMA circular · 2026-09-09

Circular on Private Market Investments

HKMA reminded registered institutions distributing publicly or privately offered products with private-credit or private-equity exposure to apply product due diligence, appropriate risk ratings, clear risk disclosure, suitability controls, monitoring and staff training. The circular states no source deadline and preserves existing institutional and qualified-corporate professional-investor exemptions.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Investment products, private-market funds
Authority / status: Hong Kong Monetary Authority — Supervisory expectation

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) issued a 9 September circular to all registered institutions on the sale and distribution of publicly or privately offered products with direct or indirect private-credit or private-equity exposure. The circular reminds registered institutions to conduct proper product due diligence and appropriate product risk rating, and to give clients sufficient, fair, balanced and non-misleading information, including on limited liquidity, transparency and valuation uncertainty. It also reminds them to ensure recommendations or solicitations are suitable, to take particular care when assessing and explaining suitability, and to maintain policies, procedures, controls, monitoring and staff training. Existing exemptions for Institutional Professional Investors and Qualified Corporate Professional Investors continue to apply; complex-product and derivative treatment depends on the relevant SFC requirements.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-03

Circular on SFC-authorised funds with exposure to private market assets

The SFC requires enhanced offering-document disclosures for affected public funds with direct or indirect exposure to private market assets. Funds with aggregate direct and indirect exposure of 50% or more of NAV will be considered complex products, and existing fund managers are expected to review funds, update documents as soon as practicable, and communicate properly with distributors.
