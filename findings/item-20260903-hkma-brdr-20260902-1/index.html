HKMA circular · 2026-09-03

Remote on-boarding of individual customers

HKMA updated supervisory expectations for remote onboarding of individual customers by authorised institutions and stored value facility licensees. The circular expects ongoing use of fraud and scam intelligence, continual solution updates and recalibration, reliable identity-document authentication, identity matching, and a comprehensive effectiveness review.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Digital onboarding, Individual accounts

The HKMA’s 3 September circular updates its supervisory expectations for remote onboarding of individual customers by authorised institutions and stored value facility licensees, replacing its 2019 individual-customer and 2021 iAM Smart circulars. It expects firms to use current fraud and scam intelligence on an ongoing basis, continually update and recalibrate remote-onboarding solutions, authenticate identity documents reliably and link the customer incontrovertibly to the identity provided. Firms should take a risk-based approach proportionate to the customer’s assessed money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk and conduct a comprehensive review of solutions, systems and oversight for ongoing effectiveness. Corporate onboarding remains governed by the separate 2020 circular, so the direct scope is individual-customer journeys; no fixed implementation date is stated.

Actions: In the next 30 days, confirm the in-scope individual journeys and compare fraud-intelligence inputs, identity authentication and matching controls, and effectiveness-review evidence with the circular; prioritise and govern any identified remediation, retaining testing, configuration, data-reconciliation and management-review evidence.
