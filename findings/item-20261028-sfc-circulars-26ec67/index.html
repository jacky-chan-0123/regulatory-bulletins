SFC supervisory circular · 2026-10-28

Supplemental circular to management companies of SFC-authorised funds on liquidity risk management

The SFC circular sets enhanced liquidity-risk-management guidance for managers of SFC-authorised open-ended funds, including expectations on liquidity assessment, liquidity-management tools, investor disclosure and reporting when specified tools or measures are activated.

⏰ Deadline: Revised UT Code effective 1 November 2026; scoped managers must enhance policies and procedures as soon as practicable and by 31 October 2027
Products: Retail funds, liquidity management, fund distribution

The SFC’s supplemental circular applies to managers of SFC-authorised open-ended funds and sets enhanced liquidity-risk-management guidance aligned to the revised UT Code. It expects managers to assess asset and portfolio liquidity against redemption terms, adopt and use appropriate liquidity-management tools and measures, and support these with systems, procedures and controls. Managers licensed by or registered with the SFC should enhance their policies and procedures as soon as practicable and no later than 31 October 2027. This is a supervisory expectation for scoped fund managers, rather than a universal banking requirement.

Actions: By the next governance cycle, confirm whether relevant entities manage, act as trustee for, or distribute scoped funds and approve the applicability decision. Then map fund policies, liquidity-management-tool readiness, stress tests, disclosures and SFC-reporting playbooks to the source-backed transition date.

SFC, AFRC and the Exchange join forces to address disclaimers of opinion solely relating to going concern

The SFC, AFRC and the Exchange issued a joint statement setting supervisory expectations for listed issuers with audit disclaimers solely relating to going concern.
