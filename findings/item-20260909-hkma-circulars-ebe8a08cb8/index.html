GS-2 Transition Planning — corrected finding

HKMA issued GS-2 V.1 on 22 September 2026 as a non-statutory SPM guidance note applicable to all authorized institutions. Circular on Private Market Investments

HKMA reminded registered institutions distributing publicly or privately offered products with private-credit or private-equity exposure to apply product due diligence, appropriate risk ratings, clear risk disclosure, suitability controls, monitoring and staff training. The circular states no source deadline and preserves existing institutional and qualified-corporate professional-investor exemptions.
