Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-09

Circular on Private Market Investments

HKMA reminded registered institutions distributing publicly or privately offered products with private-credit or private-equity exposure to apply product due diligence, appropriate risk ratings, clear risk disclosure, suitability controls, monitoring and staff training. The circular states no source deadline and preserves existing institutional and qualified-corporate professional-investor exemptions.

Implementation of T+1 Settlement Cycle for Hong Kong Cash Market

HKSCC states that, subject to market readiness and regulatory approval, Hong Kong cash-market settlement will move from T+2 to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The circular asks Clearing Participants to assess T-day post-trade operations, liquidity and funding, client cut-offs and communications, customer documents, and exception controls.

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The uncertificated securities market regime takes effect on 16 November 2026. The SFC approved six securities registrars effective that date; listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar, and existing prescribed securities constituted under Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Mainland China or Hong Kong law have five years to participate.
