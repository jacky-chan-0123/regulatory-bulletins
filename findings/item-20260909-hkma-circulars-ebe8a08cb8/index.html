HKMA has published a current circular, “Circular on Private Market Investments”, issued on 9 September 2026 for Registered Institutions. The repository classifies it under Wealth Management & MPF — Securities/investment products. The registered evidence does not expose the circular’s operative text, scope, deadline, transition, exemption or explicit expectation, so it does not establish an obligation or supervisory expectation. Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-09

Circular on Private Market Investments

HKMA reminded registered institutions distributing publicly or privately offered products with private-credit or private-equity exposure to apply product due diligence, appropriate risk ratings, clear risk disclosure, suitability controls, monitoring and staff training. The circular states no source deadline and preserves existing institutional and qualified-corporate professional-investor exemptions.
