SFC approval announcement · 2026-10-26

SFC approves first-ever secondary trading of tokenised public funds

The SFC approved secondary trading of two existing SFC-authorised tokenised money-market funds on an SFC-licensed virtual-asset trading platform, with around-the-clock trading beginning on 26 October 2026. The approval applies the April 2026 pilot framework; it is not a new sector-wide rule or direct instruction outside the approved arrangement.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Tokenised funds; virtual-asset trading; digital payments

On 26 October 2026, the Securities and Futures Commission approved secondary trading of two existing SFC-authorised tokenised money-market funds on an SFC-licensed virtual-asset trading platform, with around-the-clock trading beginning that day. The SFC states that its April 2026 pilot framework sets requirements and safeguards for trading-channel operation, fair pricing, liquidity provision and disclosure. This is the first stated live approval under that existing pilot rather than a new rule or a direct instruction to institutions outside the approved arrangement; the SFC will monitor activity and may consider broader product scope. Banks with authorised-fund, digital-asset distribution, tokenised-deposit or settlement roles may need to monitor whether a future expansion affects their product, disclosure, pricing, liquidity, settlement or 24/7 operating model.
