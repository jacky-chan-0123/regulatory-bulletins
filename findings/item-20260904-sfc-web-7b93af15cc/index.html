SFC Chief Executive speech synopsis · 2026-09-04

Powering Next Wave of Market Growth through AI and RMB Internationalisation

The SFC’s 4 September 2026 news record links to its Chief Executive’s speech synopsis, which says the SFC is considering guidance on applying existing principles to agentic-AI use cases. The synopsis identifies ownership and human oversight, controlled access and defined scope, testing and monitoring, operational resilience, and escalation and remediation as relevant outcomes, but announces no final guidance, deadline or prescribed technology design.

The Securities and Futures Commission’s 4 September 2026 news record links to its Chief Executive’s speech synopsis, which says the SFC is considering guidance on applying existing principles to agentic-AI use cases. Firms with investment-facing AI use cases may monitor subsequent SFC guidance and consider how relevant uses are governed; the scope and timing remain open.

DBS, OCBC and UOB complete first live tokenised-deposit transactions on Swift’s ledger

DBS, OCBC and UOB announced that they completed live domestic Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits on Swift’s blockchain-based ledger.
