Powering Next Wave of Market Growth through AI and RMB Internationalisation

The SFC’s 4 September 2026 news record links to its Chief Executive’s speech synopsis, which says the SFC is considering guidance on applying existing principles to agentic-AI use cases. The synopsis identifies ownership and human oversight, controlled access and defined scope, testing and monitoring, operational resilience, and escalation and remediation as relevant outcomes, but announces no final guidance, deadline or prescribed technology design.

The Securities and Futures Commission’s 4 September 2026 news record links to its Chief Executive’s speech synopsis, which says the SFC is considering guidance on applying existing principles to agentic-AI use cases. The synopsis identifies ownership and human oversight, controlled access and defined scope, testing and monitoring, operational resilience, and escalation and remediation as relevant outcomes. SFC and AFRC extend regulatory cooperation to financial reporting of licensed firms and funds under new MoU

The SFC and AFRC signed a new MoU on 28 September 2026, replacing their February 2021 MoU. It covers financial and, where applicable, compliance reporting by licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual asset service providers, SFC-authorised funds and registered open-ended fund companies, and related audit and assurance work. The MoU provides for information sharing, referrals, mutual assistance and coordinated inspections or investigations in matters of mutual regulatory interest, subject to law.
