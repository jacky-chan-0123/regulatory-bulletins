Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Indonesia Strengthen Cooperation in Cross-Border Payments

HKMA and Bank Indonesia signed an MoU to explore QR-code cross-border payment interoperability and hold technical, operational and regulatory dialogues. The announcement states no implementation arrangement, participant requirement or deadline.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Cross-border payments, QR payments, Digital

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) and Bank Indonesia signed a memorandum of understanding on 24 September 2026 to strengthen cooperation on QR-code-based cross-border payments. The authorities say they will hold technical, operational and regulatory dialogues and explore arrangements to facilitate interoperability between Hong Kong and Indonesia. The announcement is prospective and states no rollout, participant requirement, technical standard or compliance deadline. Payment providers and Hong Kong–Indonesia merchants may be affected only if a later arrangement establishes participation or interoperability; current scope remains unstated.
