Legislative Council written reply · 2026-11-04

LCQ1: Policy on the development of digital assets

In a Legislative Council reply, the Government said it and the SFC will introduce a Bill by end-2026 to extend the virtual-asset regime to dealing, advisory, management and custody. FSTB and HKMA are advancing a legislative review on wider distributed-ledger-technology use in fixed income and digital assets. The reply is a policy statement and future legislative intention, not an enacted rule.

⏰ Deadline: Government intends to introduce a Bill by end-2026; no commencement or compliance date stated
Products: Digital assets, investments, payments

In a Legislative Council reply, the Government said it and the Securities and Futures Commission will introduce a Bill by end-2026 to amend the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance and extend the virtual-asset trading-platform regime to dealing, advisory, management and custody services. The reply also says the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau and HKMA are advancing a legislative review on wider distributed-ledger-technology use in fixed income and digital assets. It describes a risk-based, technology-neutral approach, with standards aligned with and tailored from traditional financial-services and securities standards. This is a policy statement and future legislative intention, not a Bill, enacted rule, licence condition or present obligation; the legal perimeter, transition, commencement and authorised-institution implications are not stated.
