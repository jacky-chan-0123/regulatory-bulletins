parliamentary speech · 2026-09-09

Second Reading of the Scams (Countermeasures) and Other Matters Bill – Wrap-Up Speech

Singapore's Ministry of Home Affairs described proposed scam-countermeasure orders and said local banks will offer an LPBA or alternative basic account from October 2026, with no exact date stated.

⏰ Deadline: October 2026 (exact date not stated)
Products: Deposit accounts, payments

Singapore’s Ministry of Home Affairs described proposed Account Disabling, Disclosure and Service Limitation Orders in a Second Reading wrap-up speech, including information exchange with service providers and obligations imposed by those orders. It said local banks will offer an LPBA or alternative basic account from October 2026 to affected Singapore citizens or permanent residents except in limited cases, without stating an exact date. This is a legislative-stage government speech, not an enacted rule or MAS instrument, and may inform monitoring of the operational and customer-access trade-off in scam-related account restrictions without creating a Hong Kong obligation.
