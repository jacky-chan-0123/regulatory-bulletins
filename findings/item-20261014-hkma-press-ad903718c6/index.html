HKMA press release · 2026-10-14

Chongqing–Hong Kong Cross-boundary Data Validation Platform

HKMA announced the platform's launch for initial cross-boundary financial use cases, including account opening and loan applications. It verifies customer-presented data using blockchain technology and hash values without cross-boundary transfer or storage of original documents; the announcement states no participation requirement or timetable.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: SME onboarding, Lending, Cross-border data validation

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) announced the launch of the Chongqing–Hong Kong Cross-boundary Data Validation Platform on 14 October 2026. The platform verifies customer-presented data using blockchain technology and hash values without transferring or storing the original documents across the boundary. It will initially support cross-boundary financial use cases, while banks in Chongqing and Hong Kong are advancing Northbound and Southbound account-opening and loan-application use cases. The announcement states no participation requirement, implementation timetable, or prescribed standards for reliance, consent, underwriting or data protection; institutions considering participation may assess data flows, validation reliance and operator arrangements before making a commercial decision.
