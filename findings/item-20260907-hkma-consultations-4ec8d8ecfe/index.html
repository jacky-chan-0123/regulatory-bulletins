Consultation on Phase 2B prototype of Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance

HKMA opened a Phase 2B Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance consultation for all authorised institutions; comments close on 7 October 2026.

⏰ Deadline: 7 October 2026
Products: Sustainable lending, investment products

HKMA has opened a consultation for all authorised institutions on a Phase 2B prototype of the Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance. Comments close on 7 October 2026, and the repository records the consultation as open. This is a consultation rather than a binding taxonomy or new compliance requirement; sustainable lending and investment products or related claims may have product-governance, data and disclosure dependencies if the prototype is finalised.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-22

GS-2 Transition Planning — corrected finding

HKMA issued GS-2 V.1 on 22 September 2026 as a non-statutory SPM guidance note applicable to all authorized institutions. It sets supervisory expectations for governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets, stakeholder engagement, scenario analysis and communication. AIs should have transition-planning documentation in place by 1 July 2030, take active implementation steps beforehand, and be prepared to demonstrate progress 24 months after issuance.
