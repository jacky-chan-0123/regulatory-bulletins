HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

HSBC says its forthcoming Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin will be called HSBC RedCoin and is planned for a second-half 2026 launch. It says launch availability will initially be through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App for P2P and P2M payments.

⏰ Deadline: Planned launch in the second half of 2026; no specific source date stated
Products: P2P payments; merchant payments; mobile app

HSBC says its forthcoming Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin will be called HSBC RedCoin and that it has not issued stablecoins in Hong Kong. It plans a second-half 2026 launch through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App, initially for P2P and P2M payments, with potential later wholesale corporate and institutional use cases. This is an official bank statement rather than an HKMA or SFC instrument, so it does not verify licence terms, a confirmed launch date, corporate availability or cross-border scope; comparable propositions may warrant preparation on redemption communications and fraud operations.
