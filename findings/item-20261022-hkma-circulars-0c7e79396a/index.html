Anti-bribery & corruption controls for account opening

HKMA issued a circular to all authorised institutions on account-opening bribery risks. It expects layered, risk-based onboarding and proportionate customer due diligence, with segregation of duties and enhanced controls for third-party introducer cases; no source deadline is stated.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Deposit accounts, digital onboarding

HKMA’s 22 October circular follows cases in which syndicates offered staff bribes to bypass AML/CFT account-opening controls. It expects layered, risk-based onboarding and proportionate customer due diligence, with segregation of duties and enhanced controls for third-party introducer cases, including due diligence, approval scrutiny and management oversight. HKMA also calls for vulnerability gap analysis, risk-based look-backs, management information and clear senior-management responsibility. The expectation may affect branch, staff-led, digital and introducer-supported onboarding; the circular states no commencement, transition, reporting or compliance date.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-15

Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence

HKMA issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions. Annex A supersedes the 2019 AI principles and expects proportionate governance across the AI lifecycle, including ownership, inventory, risk-based testing and monitoring, recovery, records, data and third-party safeguards, and fair and transparent customer use. HKMA states that policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner; no fixed deadline is stated. Annex B provides non-exhaustive Sandbox insights, while Annex C is preliminary and does not establish current agentic-AI requirements.

Chongqing–Hong Kong Cross-boundary Data Validation Platform

HKMA announced the platform's launch for initial cross-boundary financial use cases, including account opening and loan applications.
