SFC circular on Haiti and Yemen sanctions amendments

The SFC reported that the Haiti and Yemen sanctions amendment regulations were gazetted on 11 September 2026 with immediate effect. The underlying regulations are required within their legal scope; the circular addresses licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities and does not state a new obligation for authorised institutions generally.

⏰ Deadline: Immediate effect from 2026-09-11
Products: Investment, payments, digital

On 11 September, SFC circular SFO/IS/037/2026 stated that the Haiti and Yemen amendment regulations were gazetted with immediate effect. The Haiti amendment renews travel and financial sanctions and revises arms-embargo requirements, while the Yemen amendment renews financial sanctions and a travel ban. The SFC reminds licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities to use Chapter 6 of its AML/CFT Guideline for appropriate compliance measures. The underlying regulations are required within their legal scope; the circular does not state a new obligation for authorised institutions generally.

SFC issues restriction notice to DA YUE Financial Group Limited

On 23 November 2026, the SFC issued DA YUE Financial Group Limited a restriction notice under sections 204 and 205 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Without prior written consent, the firm cannot conduct its licensed activities or deal with relevant property, subject to stated exceptions. The SFC says its initial enquiries indicate an approximately HK$29.6 million client-money shortfall and its review is ongoing.

LCQ1: Policy on the development of digital assets

In a Legislative Council reply, the Government said it and the SFC will introduce a Bill by end-2026 to extend the virtual-asset regime to dealing, advisory, management and custody.
