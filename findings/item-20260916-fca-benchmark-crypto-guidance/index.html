FCA press release on cryptoasset perimeter guidance · 2026-09-16

Crypto firms get guidance on how the new regime applies

The FCA says its future UK cryptoasset-regime guidance explains which activities may require authorisation, including qualifying-stablecoin issuance, platform operation, dealing or arranging, safeguarding and arranging staking. Applications open on 30 September 2026 and the regime is due to enter force on 25 October 2027; this is a UK benchmark, not a Hong Kong requirement.

⏰ Deadline: UK authorisation applications open 30 September 2026; UK regime enters force 25 October 2027; FCA consultation planned for October 2026
Products: investment, digital

The FCA’s 16 September press release says new guidance explains how the law underpinning the future UK cryptoasset regime applies and which activities may require FCA authorisation. The stated activity categories include qualifying-stablecoin issuance, cryptoasset trading-platform operation, dealing or arranging, safeguarding, and arranging staking. The FCA says applications open on 30 September 2026, the regime enters force on 25 October 2027, and it plans an October consultation on targeted guidance updates following Government legal changes. This is a UK perimeter-development benchmark rather than a Hong Kong requirement; UK-facing cryptoasset activity may warrant entity, product, custody-provider and customer-journey mapping, while Hong Kong-only activity is not evidence of an HK control gap.
