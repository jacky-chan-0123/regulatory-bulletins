UK financial watchdogs clarify cyber expectations as attacks rise

The FCA, PRA and Bank of England propose non-rigid guidance that consolidates existing UK requirements and supervisory lessons on cyber risk and resilience. Responses to GC26/9 close on 26 February 2027 and final guidance is expected in H1 2027. This is a UK consultation and comparable benchmark, not a Hong Kong obligation.

Products: Digital, payments

The FCA, PRA and Bank of England propose guidance that consolidates existing UK requirements and supervisory lessons on cyber risk and resilience. The proposed guidance addresses governance and identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to and recovering from cyber incidents, while remaining deliberately non-rigid. Consultation responses to GC26/9 close on 26 February 2027, and final guidance is expected in the first half of 2027. It is an analogous benchmark for Hong Kong digital and payment services, particularly where a UK-group perimeter or shared service or vendor dependency is material; it does not establish a Hong Kong control gap.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-10-19

Building the future of finance in the age of AI

In a drafted speech, the Financial Conduct Authority shared AI good and poor practice drawn from firm testing, covering customer escalation, governance, testing, monitoring, resilience and supplier dependencies. The FCA said significant AI failures and near misses should be covered by UK major-incident reporting and said it will develop an agentic-finance framework. This is a UK speech and an analogous control benchmark, not a Hong Kong obligation.

Motor finance legal challenge to be heard in February 2027

The FCA says challenges to its motor-finance compensation scheme will be heard from 16 to 26 February 2027. While the scheme is partly suspended, lenders must continue specified preparation and complaint activity; the FCA expects rigorous contingency planning, financial provisions and auditor engagement if the scheme or parts of it are quashed.

SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore, Association of Banks in Singapore · 2026-11-18

MAS and ABS Set Out PayNow Generation 2 Implementation Roadmap

MAS and ABS published a Singapore PayNow Gen2 roadmap covering staged enhancements to QR interoperability, deep-linking, selected higher-value government payments, person-to-merchant dispute arrangements, richer payment data and micropayment testing. The roadmap targets foundational standards and trials by end-2027, with staged expansion in 2028; it is a coordinated implementation roadmap rather than an immediate MAS rule.
