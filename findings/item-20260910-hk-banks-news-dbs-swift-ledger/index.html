joint bank market announcement · 2026-09-10

DBS, OCBC and UOB complete first live tokenised-deposit transactions on Swift’s ledger

DBS, OCBC and UOB announced that they completed live domestic Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits on Swift’s blockchain-based ledger. The banks say the work provides insights for round-the-clock interbank payments and may support future corporate treasury, trade and digital-commerce services; it is not a Hong Kong regulatory requirement or a confirmed customer rollout.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Payments, Digital banking

DBS says that DBS, OCBC and UOB completed live domestic Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits on Swift’s blockchain-based ledger on 10 September 2026. The banks describe this as the first live interbank use of tokenised deposits by the three Singapore banks and say it provides insights into supporting round-the-clock interbank payments beyond traditional processing windows. They identify corporate treasury, trade and digital commerce as possible future service areas, rather than confirming customer availability. This issuer-controlled market announcement is a comparable payments-infrastructure development for Hong Kong monitoring; it does not establish a Hong Kong obligation, a current control outcome or a source deadline.

SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore, Association of Banks in Singapore · 2026-11-18

MAS and ABS Set Out PayNow Generation 2 Implementation Roadmap

MAS and ABS published a Singapore PayNow Gen2 roadmap covering staged enhancements to QR interoperability, deep-linking, selected higher-value government payments, person-to-merchant dispute arrangements, richer payment data and micropayment testing. The roadmap targets foundational standards and trials by end-2027, with staged expansion in 2028; it is a coordinated implementation roadmap rather than an immediate MAS rule.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-11-16

HKMA and Swift continue collaboration on Project Ensemble and Swift ledger

HKMA and Swift are exploring cross-border, multi-currency tokenised-deposit interoperability between the Swift ledger and Project Ensemble’s settlement layer. The collaboration is a market-development update, not a participation mandate, supervisory expectation or production timetable.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-11-04

LCQ1: Policy on the development of digital assets

In a Legislative Council reply, the Government said it and the SFC will introduce a Bill by end-2026 to extend the virtual-asset regime to dealing, advisory, management and custody. FSTB and HKMA are advancing a legislative review on wider distributed-ledger-technology use in fixed income and digital assets. The reply is a policy statement and future legislative intention, not an enacted rule.
