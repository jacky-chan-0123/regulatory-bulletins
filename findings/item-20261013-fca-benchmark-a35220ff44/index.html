FCA statement · 2026-10-13

Motor finance legal challenge to be heard in February 2027

The FCA says challenges to its motor-finance compensation scheme will be heard from 16 to 26 February 2027. While the scheme is partly suspended, lenders must continue specified preparation and complaint activity; the FCA expects rigorous contingency planning, financial provisions and auditor engagement if the scheme or parts of it are quashed.

⏰ Deadline: UK Upper Tribunal hearing: 16–26 February 2027; judgment expected in the following months
Products: Lending, Complaints handling, Customer remediation

The FCA says legal challenges to its motor-finance compensation scheme will be heard by the Upper Tribunal from 16 to 26 February 2027, with judgment expected in the following months. The scheme is partly suspended, but the FCA says lenders must continue preparing for provisions that remain in force and progress complaints as far as possible, including specified consumer letters. The FCA expects lenders to plan rigorously for a possible quashing, make necessary financial provisions and engage auditors; normal complaints statutory timescales could resume in that scenario. This remains an active UK legal process and a customer-remediation and operational-readiness benchmark only, not a Hong Kong requirement.
