HKMA launches public consultation on Phase 2B prototype of Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance

HKMA is consulting on the Phase 2B prototype of the Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance, which adds 10 economic activities and includes green, transition and climate-adaptation elements. Comments are invited by 7 October 2026.

⏰ Deadline: 7 October 2026 (consultation comments)
Products: Lending, Investment, Marketing

The HKMA has opened a public consultation on the Phase 2B prototype of the Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance. The prototype adds 10 economic activities, taking the total from 25 to 39, and includes technical criteria for green and transition activities and a process-based climate-adaptation approach. Comments are invited on or before 7 October 2026. This is a consultation rather than a final taxonomy requirement; the final scope, applicability and adoption timing remain undecided.

GS-2 Transition Planning — corrected finding

HKMA issued GS-2 V.1 on 22 September 2026 as a non-statutory SPM guidance note applicable to all authorized institutions. It sets supervisory expectations for governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets, stakeholder engagement, scenario analysis and communication. AIs should have transition-planning documentation in place by 1 July 2030, take active implementation steps beforehand, and be prepared to demonstrate progress 24 months after issuance.

SFC and AFRC extend regulatory cooperation to financial reporting of licensed firms and funds under new MoU

The SFC and AFRC signed a new MoU on 28 September 2026, replacing their February 2021 MoU. It covers financial and, where applicable, compliance reporting by licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual asset service providers, SFC-authorised funds and registered open-ended fund companies, and related audit and assurance work. The MoU provides for information sharing, referrals, mutual assistance and coordinated inspections or investigations in matters of mutual regulatory interest, subject to law.
