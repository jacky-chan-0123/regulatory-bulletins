SFC circular · 2026-09-30

Obtaining client consent under the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the exchange-traded derivatives market (HKIDR-DM)

The SFC says relevant intermediaries will need express consent from individual clients for specified CID and BCAN collection and transfer under HKIDR-DM. It sets out identity-authentication, record and log expectations, while the proposed Code provision remains to be gazetted and its effective date is to be determined by the SFC.

⏰ Deadline: Q2 2028 scheduled launch; Code effective date to be determined
Products: Listed derivatives, client onboarding, securities operations

The SFC sets out forthcoming supervisory expectations for relevant intermediaries to obtain express consent from individual clients for specified CID and BCAN collection and transfer under HKIDR-DM. Consent cannot be inferred; without it, an intermediary may not enter an order or trade that opens a position, although it may enter an order or trade that closes an existing listed-derivatives position. The circular calls for identity authentication, retained consent and withdrawal records, and a written log retained during the client relationship and for at least two years afterwards. Paragraph 5.6A is still to be gazetted and will take effect on an SFC-determined date, so the stated Q2 2028 schedule is not a current legal effective date and the individual-consent requirement does not extend to corporate clients.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-30

Roadmap for Implementing the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the Exchange-traded Derivatives Market

The SFC roadmap schedules HKIDR-DM implementation for Q2 2028, subject to market readiness, testing and rehearsals, and sets preparation milestones for relevant licensed corporations and registered institutions. The proposed Code provision remains to be gazetted and will take effect on a future SFC-determined date.

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The SFC approved six securities registrars effective 16 November 2026, when the USM regime takes effect. Listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar; the stated requirements apply to issuers and registrars, not every bank.
