AML/CFT sanctions circular · 2026-11-17

Circular on DPRK sanctions-list update

The SFC reported an amendment to a DPRK sanctions-list entity record and publication of the updated Hong Kong list. It expects licensed firms and their associated entities to screen new or revised designations against client lists as soon as practicable whenever there are updates.

⏰ Deadline: As soon as practicable whenever there are updates
Products: Investment services; virtual-asset services

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) says the UN Security Council DPRK Sanctions Committee amended an entity record on 13 November 2026 (New York time), and that an updated Hong Kong list under section 31 of Cap. 537AE was published on 17 November. The circular is addressed to licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and their associated entities, and the SFC expects them to screen all new or revised designations against client lists as soon as practicable whenever there are updates, under its AML/CFT Guideline. This is a sanctions-data update rather than a new sanctions regime; direct applicability to an authorised institution outside the stated SFC population is not established by the circular. In-scope operations may need to verify data-feed deployment, alert handling and retained screening evidence for the amended record.

Source: Circular to Licensed Corporations, SFC-licensed Virtual Asset Service Providers and Associated Entities — Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Financing of Terrorism: United Nations Sanctions (Democratic People’s Republic of Korea) Regulation

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-11-23

SFC issues restriction notice to DA YUE Financial Group Limited

On 23 November 2026, the SFC issued DA YUE Financial Group Limited a restriction notice under sections 204 and 205 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Without prior written consent, the firm cannot conduct its licensed activities or deal with relevant property, subject to stated exceptions. The SFC says its initial enquiries indicate an approximately HK$29.6 million client-money shortfall and its review is ongoing.

Anti-bribery & corruption controls for account opening
