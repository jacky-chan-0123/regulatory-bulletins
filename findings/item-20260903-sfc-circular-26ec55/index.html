SFC circular · 2026-09-03

Circular on SFC-authorised funds with exposure to private market assets

The SFC requires enhanced offering-document disclosures for affected public funds with direct or indirect exposure to private market assets. Funds with aggregate direct and indirect exposure of 50% or more of NAV will be considered complex products, and existing fund managers are expected to review funds, update documents as soon as practicable, and communicate properly with distributors.

⏰ Deadline: Existing funds: take appropriate action, including offering-document updates, as soon as practicable; no fixed source deadline stated
Products: Investment funds, Investment distribution

The SFC’s 3 September circular applies to managers of SFC-authorised funds offered to the public in Hong Kong that have direct or indirect exposure to private market assets. It requires enhanced offering-document disclosures on the extent and means of exposure, underlying-asset characteristics, and key risks, including in the key facts statement where appropriate. A fund with aggregate direct and indirect exposure of 50% or more of NAV will be considered a complex product, while the SFC may designate a fund below that threshold case by case. For existing funds, managers are expected to review the funds, update offering documents as soon as practicable and ensure necessary and proper distributor communications; where a bank manages or distributes affected funds, product inventory, product-risk classification and suitability controls may be affected.
