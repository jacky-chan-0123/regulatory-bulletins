Northern Metropolis Financial Advisory Taskforce holds its second meeting

HKMA and HKAB announced continuing work on financing models and risk mitigants for Northern Metropolis projects; this is a prospective commercial opportunity rather than a financing requirement or commitment.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Project finance; ancillary financial services

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) and Hong Kong Association of Banks (HKAB) convened the Taskforce’s second meeting to explore financing models for Northern Metropolis projects. The Government said approved funding for related public works exceeded HK$180 billion as at July 2026, and presented industry-park-company and public-private partnership (PPP) development models. A dedicated PPP Financing Workstream will examine Mainland and overseas cases, development models, risk-mitigation measures and complementary arrangements, including financing templates for banks’ reference. The update signals a prospective lending and ancillary-services opportunity for banks with relevant appetite, but does not establish a requirement, prescribed credit control, project availability, bank commitment or timetable.
