SFC press release · 2026-10-12

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The SFC approved six securities registrars effective 16 November 2026, when the USM regime takes effect. Listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar; the stated requirements apply to issuers and registrars, not every bank.

⏰ Deadline: 16 November 2026; existing issuers must participate within five years
Products: Investment, Custody

The SFC approved six companies as approved securities registrars with effect from 16 November 2026, when the USM regime takes effect. It identifies Computershare Hong Kong Investor Services Limited and Tricor Investor Services Limited as the first approved for USM-related services. Listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar, while post-16 November listing applicants with uncertificated securities must appoint one approved for USM-related services. A listed group entity, custodian or investment-services business may need to assess registrar appointments, register and settlement dependencies, and client-servicing implications; the announcement does not create a direct duty for every bank.
