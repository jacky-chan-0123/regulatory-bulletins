SFC circular and implementation roadmap · 2026-09-30

Roadmap for Implementing the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the Exchange-traded Derivatives Market

The SFC roadmap schedules HKIDR-DM implementation for Q2 2028, subject to market readiness, testing and rehearsals, and sets preparation milestones for relevant licensed corporations and registered institutions. The proposed Code provision remains to be gazetted and will take effect on a future SFC-determined date.

⏰ Deadline: Preparation from Q3 2026; Q2 2028 scheduled launch subject to readiness
Products: Listed derivatives, securities operations, client onboarding

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) signals a future HKIDR-DM implementation programme for relevant licensed corporations and registered institutions, with a Q2 2028 launch scheduled subject to market readiness, testing and rehearsals. Its roadmap sets preparation periods for consent and CID updates through Q4 2027, OMS enhancement to Q3 2027, and BCAN/mapping-file preparation, testing and submission through Q1 2028. The proposed Code provision will be gazetted and take effect on a future SFC-determined date, so the roadmap does not fix commencement or replace future HKEX technical requirements. In-scope businesses may need coordinated client-data, OMS, broker-chain, data-lineage and testing work; corporate CID is in scope, while the individual-consent requirement does not apply to corporate clients.

Obtaining client consent under the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the exchange-traded derivatives market (HKIDR-DM)

The SFC says relevant intermediaries will need express consent from individual clients for specified CID and BCAN collection and transfer under HKIDR-DM. It sets out identity-authentication, record and log expectations, while the proposed Code provision remains to be gazetted and its effective date is to be determined by the SFC.
