SFC issues restriction notice to DA YUE Financial Group Limited

Products: Investment services, futures

On 23 November 2026, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) issued DA YUE Financial Group Limited (DYFGL) a restriction notice under sections 204 and 205 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Without the SFC’s prior written consent, DYFGL cannot conduct the regulated activities for which it is licensed or deal with relevant property, subject to stated exceptions for deposits to its account with an authorized financial institution and consented ordinary-course expenses. The SFC says its initial enquiries indicate an approximately HK$29.6 million client-money shortfall, including client money used for other clients’ futures-margin requirements and trading losses; around 270 active clients hold credit balances. The SFC’s review is ongoing, so the stated concerns are not final findings; for Hong Kong bank securities businesses, the notice is an analogous client-asset and futures-margin control signal where an actual DYFGL relationship exists, rather than a new general requirement.
