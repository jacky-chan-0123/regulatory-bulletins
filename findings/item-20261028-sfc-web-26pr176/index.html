SFC guidance announcement · 2026-10-28

SFC issues guidance to support enhancements to Hong Kong’s retail fund regime

The SFC announced guidance, FAQs, updated application guides and key facts statement templates to support the revised UT Code, covering derivatives exposure, liquidity risk, money market funds, disclosures and transition arrangements.

⏰ Deadline: Revised UT Code effective 1 November 2026; specified changes are subject to a 12-month transitional period
Products: Retail funds, fund distribution, disclosures

The SFC announced a guidance package supporting the revised UT Code, which takes effect on 1 November 2026. The package covers circulars, FAQs and updated application guides on derivatives Value-at-Risk, liquidity risk, money-market-fund investment and disclosure, and transitional arrangements. It signals a concurrent applicability and implementation review for entities that manage, distribute, administer or provide custody operations for SFC-authorised retail funds. The announcement sets out regulatory expectations and clarifies some existing practices, but is not itself the operative rule for every named topic or entity.
