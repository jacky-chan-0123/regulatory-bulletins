FCA press release · 2026-09-30

FCA opens the gateway to regulated crypto

FCA opened applications for UK crypto-firm authorisation. Firms intending to continue operating in the UK should apply by 28 February 2027, ahead of the new regime taking effect on 25 October 2027.

⏰ Deadline: UK continuation applications: 28 February 2027; UK regime commencement: 25 October 2027
Products: Cryptoassets; digital payments; investments

FCA opened applications on 30 September 2026 for UK crypto-firm authorisation and says firms intending to continue operating in the UK should apply by 28 February 2027, ahead of the new regime taking effect on 25 October 2027. FCA says applicants must demonstrate standards on consumer protection, safeguarding customer assets, market integrity and financial resilience, while existing firms applying during the period may continue services as an undecided application is assessed. These are UK requirements rather than Hong Kong obligations; for a group entity operating in the UK, the announcement is an authorisation-readiness trigger, while for Hong Kong it is only an analogous benchmark.
