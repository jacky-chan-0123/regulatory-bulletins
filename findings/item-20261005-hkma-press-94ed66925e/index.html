HKMA hosts CargoX Solution Day to promote regional trade data connectivity

HKMA reported that CargoX Connect links the Commercial Data Interchange with a Shanghai Data Bureau digital-trade platform, enabling five pilot banks to use Yangtze River Delta trade-declaration data for trade verification and counterparty due diligence. The programme update reports ten approved trade-finance loans totalling HK$180 million and an intended extension to other Mainland locations; the release does not state a participation mandate or new control standard.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: SME trade lending, digital trade data, cross-border trade finance

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) reported that it held a CargoX Solution Day and signed a memorandum of understanding with the PAA Digital Trade Alliance to promote trade-finance digitalisation. HKMA said CargoX Connect links the Commercial Data Interchange with a Shanghai Data Bureau digital-trade platform, enabling five pilot banks to use Yangtze River Delta trade-declaration data for trade verification and counterparty due diligence. HKMA says the pilot has enabled approval of ten trade-finance loans totalling HK$180 million, and intends to extend the model to other Mainland provinces and cities. This is a market-infrastructure programme update rather than a new requirement or supervisory expectation; the release does not state a participation mandate, data-access terms, customer-consent conditions, underwriting criteria or control standards.
