The Chief Executive's 2026 Policy Address — International Financial Centre

The Policy Address says the HKMA will introduce a seven-day offshore RMB liquidity tender mechanism, explore offshore RMB short-term debt instruments, and encourage more foreign banks in Hong Kong to join CIPS directly. It is market-development direction, not a stated binding requirement or timetable for authorised institutions.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: FX, investment, cross-border-GBA

The 16 September Policy Address says the HKMA will introduce a tendering mechanism for seven-day offshore RMB liquidity to broaden banks’ short-term-financing channels. It also says the HKMA will explore offshore RMB short-term debt instruments for investment and liquidity management, and encourage more foreign banks in Hong Kong to join CIPS directly. This is Government policy and market-development direction, not a binding rule or HKMA supervisory guideline; the source states no timetable, eligibility, terms, reporting duty or participation condition. Treasury and payments teams may need to identify relevant RMB funding, investment and CIPS-connectivity dependencies as implementation details emerge, while formal HKMA terms remain the decision point.
