The Chief Executive's 2026 Policy Address — International Financial Centre

The Policy Address says the HKMA will introduce a seven-day offshore RMB liquidity tender mechanism, explore offshore RMB short-term debt instruments, and encourage more foreign banks in Hong Kong to join CIPS directly. It is market-development direction, not a stated binding requirement or timetable for authorised institutions.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: FX, investment, cross-border-GBA

The 16 September Policy Address says the HKMA will introduce a tendering mechanism for seven-day offshore RMB liquidity to broaden banks’ short-term-financing channels. It also says the HKMA will explore offshore RMB short-term debt instruments for investment and liquidity management, and encourage more foreign banks in Hong Kong to join CIPS directly. This is Government policy and market-development direction, not a binding rule or HKMA supervisory guideline; the source states no timetable, eligibility, terms, reporting duty or participation condition. Treasury and payments teams may need to identify relevant RMB funding, investment and CIPS-connectivity dependencies as implementation details emerge, while formal HKMA terms remain the decision point.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority, Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-08

Record year for investment product sales in Hong Kong with robust demand for FICC-related products: SFC-HKMA joint survey 2025

The HKMA and SFC reported that 2025 non-exchange-traded investment-product sales reached HK$9.9 trillion, up 63% year on year; online sales were 21% of aggregate transaction value and currency-linked product sales were HK$698 billion. The release reports survey results and states no new deadline or requirement.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-09-16

Crypto firms get guidance on how the new regime applies

The FCA says its future UK cryptoasset-regime guidance explains which activities may require authorisation, including qualifying-stablecoin issuance, platform operation, dealing or arranging, safeguarding and arranging staking. Applications open on 30 September 2026 and the regime is due to enter force on 25 October 2027; this is a UK benchmark, not a Hong Kong requirement.

SFC circular on Haiti and Yemen sanctions amendments

The SFC reported that the Haiti and Yemen sanctions amendment regulations were gazetted on 11 September 2026 with immediate effect. The underlying regulations are required within their legal scope; the circular addresses licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities and does not state a new obligation for authorised institutions generally.
