government written reply to Legislative Council question · 2026-10-07

LCQ17: Accelerated settlement for cash market

The Hong Kong Government says HKEX is preparing a proposed T+1 cash-market settlement cycle and targets consultation conclusions and implementation arrangements within 2026; no final implementation date or participant requirements are stated.

⏰ Deadline: HKEX targets consultation conclusions and implementation arrangements within 2026; no final implementation date stated
Products: Securities settlement, custody, investment services

The Government’s Legislative Council reply says HKEX is preparing with the market for a proposed T+1 cash-market settlement cycle, subject to co-ordination with relevant Mainland units. HKEX targets consultation conclusions and implementation arrangements within 2026 and is considering revised clearing and settlement timetables, longer settlement-instruction hours, allocation and confirmation tools, and a stakeholder workflow platform. It says specifications and guidance will follow, with preparation and testing time; the reply does not confirm an implementation date or participant requirements. Banks acting as brokers, custodians, fund managers or settlement participants may be affected through post-trade cut-offs, funding, client confirmation, systems and vendor dependencies.

Implementation of T+1 Settlement Cycle for Hong Kong Cash Market

HKSCC states that, subject to market readiness and regulatory approval, Hong Kong cash-market settlement will move from T+2 to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The circular asks Clearing Participants to assess T-day post-trade operations, liquidity and funding, client cut-offs and communications, customer documents, and exception controls.
