Participating banks in IP Financing Sandbox complete credit approval for first batch of pilot cases

HKMA reports that three participating banks completed credit approvals for seven IP Financing Sandbox pilot cases, using independent IP valuation reports in credit assessment. HKMA describes the Sandbox as a risk-controlled pilot and does not state a broader participation requirement or a new supervisory deadline.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: SME lending, IP-backed finance

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) reports that three participating banks completed credit approvals for seven IP Financing Sandbox pilots, with loans from HK$1 million to HK$39 million for working capital, expansion and market promotion. The Sandbox, launched in December 2025 with government bodies and supported by HKAB, uses independent IP valuation reports in credit assessment and is described as a risk-controlled pilot. This is an implementation option and voluntary product-development reference, not a new supervisory obligation, mandated valuation standard or participation requirement. Banks considering comparable IP-backed SME lending may need to assess credit policy, valuation independence, collateral enforceability, fraud and conflict risk, customer disclosure, and third-party valuation capability; pilot outcomes and operating effectiveness are not stated.
