Circular · 2026-11-09

Amendments to HKSCC Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI Terms and Conditions

HKSCC states that SFC-approved amendments effective 16 November 2026 introduce USI transfer role, identity and beneficial-interest check, six-year record-retention and ASR-fee provisions for affected Uncertificated Securities Market participants.

⏰ Deadline: 16 November 2026
Products: Investment, securities settlement

HKSCC states that SFC-approved amendments to its Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI terms take effect on 16 November 2026 in support of the Uncertificated Securities Market and revised fees. For participating securities, only Stamp Authorized Persons may submit USI transfer requests, and they must perform specified identity and beneficial-interest checks and retain supporting records for at least six years. The circular also provides for ASR fees payable by non-registrar participants, including through EPI. Affected CCASS participants, Stamp Authorized Persons, Investor Participants and outsourced securities-operations providers should determine role and security scope, then prepare the related workflow, evidence-retention and fee-reconciliation changes.

HK · Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited · 2026-10-16

Implementation of T+1 Settlement Cycle for Hong Kong Cash Market

HKSCC states that, subject to market readiness and regulatory approval, Hong Kong cash-market settlement will move from T+2 to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The circular asks Clearing Participants to assess T-day post-trade operations, liquidity and funding, client cut-offs and communications, customer documents, and exception controls.

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The SFC approved six securities registrars effective 16 November 2026, when the USM regime takes effect. Listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar; the stated requirements apply to issuers and registrars, not every bank.
