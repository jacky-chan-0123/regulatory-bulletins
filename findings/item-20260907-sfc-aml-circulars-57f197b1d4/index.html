AML/CFT early-alert circular · 2026-09-07

Early Alert on United Nations Sanctions

The SFC expects licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities to update customer- and payment-screening databases for two UN sanctions-list amendments. It states no deadline.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Investment, Payments, Digital

The SFC alerts licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities to amendments made to two individual entries on the UN Security Council committee’s ISIL (Da’esh) and Al-Qaida sanctions list. It expects those entities to update customer- and payment-screening databases, with reference to the SFC’s 2018 circular on expected regulatory standards. An updated list under section 25 of Cap. 537CB was published on 6 September 2026, and the SFC says the entities will be alerted when the Cap. 575 list is gazetted. The alert expresses a supervisory expectation for its named perimeter and does not evidence a new prudential rule for authorised institutions generally.

Source: Circular to Licensed Corporations, SFC-licensed Virtual Asset Service Providers and Associated Entities — Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Financing of Terrorism — Early Alert on United Nations Sanctions
