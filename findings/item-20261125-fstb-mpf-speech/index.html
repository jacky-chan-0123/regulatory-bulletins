Legislative Council Second Reading speech · 2026-11-25

Speech by SFST in moving the Second Reading of Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026

The Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury moved the Second Reading of the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026. The speech describes proposed changes to recovery of overdue mandatory contributions, MPF trustee discipline, MPF intermediary misconduct and MPF fund investment rules. It does not state that the Bill has been passed or provide a commencement date.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: MPF administration, retirement investment, employer contributions

The Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury moved the Second Reading of the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026 on 25 November, so the measures described remain proposals rather than enacted obligations. The speech proposes a two-tier surcharge for overdue mandatory contributions, including a 10% surcharge where arrears exceed 120 days, and a power for the MPFA to require suspected defaulting employers to provide employee-income information within a specified period. It also proposes trustee fines and/or public reprimands that cannot be passed to schemes, constituent funds or members, disciplinary action for MPF-intermediary misconduct, and investment-rule flexibility. Bank-affiliated MPF trustees or intermediaries, and group employers, may need to monitor the Bill and conditionally map arrears, notice-data, disciplinary-governance and member-charge processes; no commencement or compliance date is stated.
