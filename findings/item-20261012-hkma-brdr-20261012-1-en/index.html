HKMA circular and practice guide · 2026-10-12

Practice Guide on Data Excellence

HKMA issued a Practice Guide on Data Excellence to all Authorized Institutions. It expects AIs to take its industry good practices into account when strengthening risk-data management across governance and accountability, data-quality management and control, and data infrastructure; the Guide creates no separate prescriptive requirement or mandated model.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-15

Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence

HKMA issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions. Annex A supersedes the 2019 AI principles and expects proportionate governance across the AI lifecycle, including ownership, inventory, risk-based testing and monitoring, recovery, records, data and third-party safeguards, and fair and transparent customer use. HKMA states that policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner; no fixed deadline is stated. Annex B provides non-exhaustive Sandbox insights, while Annex C is preliminary and does not establish current agentic-AI requirements.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-14

Chongqing–Hong Kong Cross-boundary Data Validation Platform

HKMA announced the platform's launch for initial cross-boundary financial use cases, including account opening and loan applications. It verifies customer-presented data using blockchain technology and hash values without cross-boundary transfer or storage of original documents; the announcement states no participation requirement or timetable.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-06

Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Negara Malaysia Strengthen Bilateral Cooperation

HKMA and Bank Negara Malaysia announced bilateral workstreams on compliant cross-border stablecoin use cases, tokenisation and digital money, SME trade-finance data, cyber resilience, RTGS connectivity and possible central securities depository linkage. The announcement states collaboration and exploration, not a participation mandate, technical specification or implementation timetable for authorised institutions.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-10-21

LCQ1: Improving online bank account opening

A Legislative Council reply reports existing HKMA expectations for remote account-opening identity verification, control enhancement, ongoing due diligence, transaction monitoring and suspicious-transaction reporting. It also reports first-phase iAM Smart step-up-authentication testing; any expansion remains subject to HKMA’s assessment of security, stability and customer experience on a risk-based approach.
