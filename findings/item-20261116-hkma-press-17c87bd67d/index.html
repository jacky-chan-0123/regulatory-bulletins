HKMA and Swift continue collaboration on Project Ensemble and Swift ledger

HKMA and Swift are exploring cross-border, multi-currency tokenised-deposit interoperability between the Swift ledger and Project Ensemble’s settlement layer. The collaboration is a market-development update, not a participation mandate, supervisory expectation or production timetable.

⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Payments; Digital; Cross-border/GBA

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) announced a strengthened collaboration with Swift to explore cross-border, multi-currency tokenised-deposit interoperability between the Swift ledger and Project Ensemble’s settlement layer. HKMA named Bank of China (Hong Kong), Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong) and HSBC in the collaboration and said the work aims to support real-time, always-on and programmable transactions. This is a market-development update, not a rule, consultation, participation mandate or supervisory expectation, and the release does not evidence production deployment. Banks considering these use cases may need to assess entity and booking location, customer use case, settlement, data, resilience, and Swift/Project Ensemble dependencies before deciding whether to explore them.
