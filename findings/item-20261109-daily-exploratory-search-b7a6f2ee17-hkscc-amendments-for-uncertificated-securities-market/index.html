Amendments to HKSCC Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI Terms and Conditions

HKSCC states that the SFC approved amendments to its rules, procedures and FINI terms for the Uncertificated Securities Market initiative. The amendments take effect on 16 November 2026 and include defined participant controls for uncertificated securities transfers and ASR-fee arrangements.

⏰ Deadline: 16 November 2026
Products: Investments, securities settlement

HKSCC states that the SFC has approved amendments to its General Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI Terms and Conditions to support Hong Kong’s Uncertificated Securities Market initiative and modernise the fee structure. The amendments take effect on 16 November 2026 and apply to the defined HKSCC participant population, rather than establishing an HKMA requirement for all authorised institutions. For participating securities in uncertificated form, only Stamp Authorized Persons may submit USI Transfer Requests; they must complete specified matching and beneficial-interest checks and retain records demonstrating compliance for at least six years. The circular also introduces ASR fees for prescribed securities, payable by participants other than registrar participants, with EPI included among the payment mechanisms.

Source: Amendments to the General Rules of HKSCC, the HKSCC Operational Procedures and the Terms and Conditions of FINI

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-12

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The uncertificated securities market regime takes effect on 16 November 2026. The SFC approved six securities registrars effective that date; listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar, and existing prescribed securities constituted under Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Mainland China or Hong Kong law have five years to participate.
