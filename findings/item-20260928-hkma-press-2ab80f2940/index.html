HKMA/HKAB market announcement of industry-agreed credit support · 2026-09-28

Banking industry further extends loan repayment grace period for affected individuals of Tai Po Fire

HKMA and HKAB announced that all 28 retail banks will extend by a further six months, to end-May 2027, the principal-and-interest repayment grace period for affected individuals’ existing mortgages, personal loans and credit-card loans. Wang Fuk Court mortgagee banks will continue flexible mortgage handling, including extending the original-mortgage grace period until a newly purchased unit is ready for intake.

⏰ Deadline: End of May 2027 for the further six-month repayment grace period
Products: Mortgages, personal loans, credit cards

The HKMA and HKAB announced that all 28 retail banks will extend by a further six months, to end-May 2027, the principal-and-interest repayment grace period for affected individuals’ existing mortgages, personal loans and credit-card loans. Wang Fuk Court mortgagee banks will continue to handle mortgages flexibly, including extending the original-mortgage grace period until a newly purchased unit is ready for intake. The announcement is an industry-agreed credit-support measure rather than a new binding HKMA rule. It does not state eligibility evidence, accounting or credit-reporting treatment, fees, or arrangements for SME borrowers.

More from Hong Kong Monetary Authority

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-15

Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence

HKMA issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions. Annex A supersedes the 2019 AI principles and expects proportionate governance across the AI lifecycle, including ownership, inventory, risk-based testing and monitoring, recovery, records, data and third-party safeguards, and fair and transparent customer use. HKMA states that policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner; no fixed deadline is stated. Annex B provides non-exhaustive Sandbox insights, while Annex C is preliminary and does not establish current agentic-AI requirements.
