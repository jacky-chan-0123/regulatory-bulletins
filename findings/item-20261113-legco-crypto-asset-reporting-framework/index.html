The LegCo record states that the Inland Revenue amendment Bill passed on 4 November 2026, with Administration amendments, and that the resulting ordinance was gazetted on 13 November 2026. The retrieved record establishes enactment and gazettal only; it does not provide the enacted text, commencement, in-scope reporting entities, due-diligence steps, reporting fields, thresholds, exemptions or transition. Entities offering crypto-asset services or holding potentially reportable customer and transaction data may need tax-reporting, onboarding, data-lineage, reporting and vendor assessment once the operative instrument is confirmed. Tax, Legal and Digital Assets should obtain the gazetted ordinance and commencement provisions before assigning implementation.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-11-04

Government welcomes passage of Inland Revenue (Amendment) (Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and Amended Common Reporting Standard) Bill 2026

The Government announced that the Legislative Council passed the Bill to implement OECD CARF and amended CRS. Relevant service providers will collect CARF-specified information in 2027 for IRD exchanges from 2028; amended CRS takes effect in 2028 and exchanges begin in 2029. IRD will issue guidance and provide technical support.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-11-04

LCQ1: Policy on the development of digital assets

In a Legislative Council reply, the Government said it and the SFC will introduce a Bill by end-2026 to extend the virtual-asset regime to dealing, advisory, management and custody. FSTB and HKMA are advancing a legislative review on wider distributed-ledger-technology use in fixed income and digital assets. The reply is a policy statement and future legislative intention, not an enacted rule.
