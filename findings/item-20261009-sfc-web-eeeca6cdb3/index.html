SFC speech · 2026-10-09

Project Liquidity: Engineering the Market Plumbing that Unlocks Capital Flows for Hong Kong

The SFC published a speech describing implemented and prospective HKEX clearing, collateral and settlement initiatives, including a March 2027 client-margin phase-2 target subject to regulatory approval. It is an official market-development signal, not a binding direction; final HKEX approvals, scope and terms remain to be published.

⏰ Deadline: November 2026 for prospective Mainland-bond collateral extension; March 2027 client-margin phase 2 target, subject to regulatory approval
Products: Derivatives clearing; Securities collateral; Custody

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) published a 9 October speech describing market-infrastructure work rather than a rule, circular or binding direction. It reports that the first phase reducing the HKEX derivatives-clearing-house client-margin multiplier from 1.33 to 1.2 was implemented in September 2026, while a reduction to 1.1 is targeted for March 2027 subject to regulatory approval. The speech also signals work towards November 2026 use of specified Mainland bonds as margin collateral at HKEX’s largest futures and options clearing houses, and OTC Clear’s aim to enable same-day settlement for non-cash collateral during 2026. Clearing participants, brokers, custodians and banks providing derivatives, treasury or securities-collateral services may need to assess margin, collateral eligibility, custody, settlement and client-communication dependencies once final HKEX materials define scope and terms.

Actions: Securities Operations and Treasury should determine whether the institution is in scope and track the cited HKEX publications. Where applicable, map collateral, margin, custody and settlement changes and retain decision, configuration, reconciliation and testing evidence; Risk and Compliance should challenge the assessment.

Source: New speech by Julia Leung: Keynote address at ASIFMA’s Future of Liquidity in Asia Pacific Capital Markets Conference
