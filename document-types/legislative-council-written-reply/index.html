HK · Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau · 2026-11-11

LCQ14: Building international gold trading market

The government reported that the gold clearing and settlement system is in trial operation with 11 provisional direct-participant banks and is planned to launch in Q1 2027. It is to connect with RTGS for delivery-versus-payment before launch; no bank participation requirement or compliance deadline is stated.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-11-04

LCQ1: Policy on the development of digital assets

In a Legislative Council reply, the Government said it and the SFC will introduce a Bill by end-2026 to extend the virtual-asset regime to dealing, advisory, management and custody. FSTB and HKMA are advancing a legislative review on wider distributed-ledger-technology use in fixed income and digital assets. The reply is a policy statement and future legislative intention, not an enacted rule.
