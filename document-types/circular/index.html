HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-11-19

Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks

HKMA's circular to all authorized institutions introduces HKAB's guideline. HKMA expects assessment against its good practices, enhancements where appropriate, and a compliance-status report by end-Q3 2027. The circular describes the guideline as a consolidated reference that reinforces existing regulations and statutory requirements.

HK · Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited · 2026-11-09

Amendments to HKSCC Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI Terms and Conditions

HKSCC states that SFC-approved amendments effective 16 November 2026 introduce USI transfer role, identity and beneficial-interest check, six-year record-retention and ASR-fee provisions for affected Uncertificated Securities Market participants.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited (HKSCC) · 2026-11-09

Amendments to HKSCC Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI Terms and Conditions

HKSCC states that the SFC approved amendments to its rules, procedures and FINI terms for the Uncertificated Securities Market initiative. The amendments take effect on 16 November 2026 and include defined participant controls for uncertificated securities transfers and ASR-fee arrangements.
