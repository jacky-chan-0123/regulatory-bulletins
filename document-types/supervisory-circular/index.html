HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-15

Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence

HKMA issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions. Annex A supersedes the 2019 AI principles and expects proportionate governance across the AI lifecycle, including ownership, inventory, risk-based testing and monitoring, recovery, records, data and third-party safeguards, and fair and transparent customer use. HKMA states that policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner; no fixed deadline is stated. Annex B provides non-exhaustive Sandbox insights, while Annex C is preliminary and does not establish current agentic-AI requirements.
