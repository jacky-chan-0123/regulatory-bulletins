🟡 SFC circular on Haiti and Yemen sanctions amendments 🇭🇰

Item ID: item-20260911-sfc-aml-circulars-26ec57
⏰ Deadline: Immediate effect from 2026-09-11
Products: Investment, payments, digital

On 11 September, SFC circular SFO/IS/037/2026 stated that the Haiti and Yemen amendment regulations were gazetted with immediate effect. The Haiti amendment renews travel and financial sanctions and revises arms-embargo requirements, while the Yemen amendment renews financial sanctions and a travel ban. The SFC reminds licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities to use Chapter 6 of its AML/CFT Guideline for appropriate compliance measures. The underlying regulations are required within their legal scope; the circular does not state a new obligation for authorised institutions generally.

Source: SFC Circular SFO/IS/037/2026
