Executive Summary

Hong Kong


🟡 Banking industry further extends loan repayment grace period for affected individuals of Tai Po Fire 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260928-hkma-press-2ab80f2940
⏰ Deadline: End of May 2027 for the further six-month repayment grace period
Products: Mortgages, personal loans, credit cards

The HKMA and HKAB announced that all 28 retail banks will extend by a further six months, to end-May 2027, the principal-and-interest repayment grace period for affected individuals’ existing mortgages, personal loans and credit-card loans. Wang Fuk Court mortgagee banks will continue to handle mortgages flexibly, including extending the original-mortgage grace period until a newly purchased unit is ready for intake. The announcement is an industry-agreed credit-support measure rather than a new binding HKMA rule. It does not state eligibility evidence, accounting or credit-reporting treatment, fees, or arrangements for SME borrowers.

Source: Banking industry further extends loan repayment grace period for affected individuals of Tai Po Fire
Primary source: official document


🟡 SFC and AFRC extend regulatory cooperation to financial reporting of licensed firms and funds under new MoU 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260928-sfc-web-26pr156
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Investment services, virtual-asset services

The SFC and AFRC signed a new MoU on 28 September 2026, replacing their February 2021 MoU. It covers financial and, where applicable, compliance reporting by licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual asset service providers, SFC-authorised funds and registered open-ended fund companies, as well as related audit and assurance work. The MoU provides for information sharing, referrals, mutual assistance and coordinated inspections or investigations in matters of mutual regulatory interest, subject to law, and took effect when signed. It is a non-binding statement of intent that does not modify law or create enforceable third-party obligations; its relevance is to existing reporting and audit evidence for entities within the stated scope.
