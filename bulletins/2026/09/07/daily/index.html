Products: Investment services; Digital channels

The Securities and Futures Commission’s 4 September 2026 news record links to its Chief Executive’s speech synopsis, which says the SFC is considering guidance on applying existing principles to agentic-AI use cases. The synopsis identifies ownership and human oversight, controlled access and defined scope, testing and monitoring, operational resilience, and escalation and remediation as relevant outcomes. This is a prospective supervisory signal, not final guidance, a new rule or a prescribed technology design. Firms with investment-facing AI use cases may monitor subsequent SFC guidance and consider how relevant uses are governed; the scope and timing remain open.
