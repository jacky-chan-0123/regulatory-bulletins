🟡 DBS, OCBC and UOB complete first live tokenised-deposit transactions on Swift’s ledger 🇸🇬
Item ID: item-20260910-hk-banks-news-dbs-swift-ledger
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Payments, Digital banking

DBS says that DBS, OCBC and UOB completed live domestic Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits on Swift’s blockchain-based ledger on 10 September 2026. The banks describe this as the first live interbank use of tokenised deposits by the three Singapore banks and say it provides insights into supporting round-the-clock interbank payments beyond traditional processing windows. They identify corporate treasury, trade and digital commerce as possible future service areas, rather than confirming customer availability. This issuer-controlled market announcement is a comparable payments-infrastructure development for Hong Kong monitoring; it does not establish a Hong Kong obligation, a current control outcome or a source deadline.

Source: DBS, OCBC and UOB complete first live blockchain-enabled transactions on Swift’s ledger
