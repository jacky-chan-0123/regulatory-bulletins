🟡 Consultation on Phase 2B prototype of Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance 🇭🇰

Item ID: item-20260907-hkma-consultations-4ec8d8ecfe
⏰ Deadline: 7 October 2026
Products: Sustainable lending, investment products

HKMA has opened a consultation for all authorised institutions on a Phase 2B prototype of the Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance. Comments close on 7 October 2026, and the repository records the consultation as open. This is a consultation rather than a binding taxonomy or new compliance requirement; sustainable lending and investment products or related claims may have product-governance, data and disclosure dependencies if the prototype is finalised.

Source: Consultation on Phase 2B prototype of Hong Kong Taxonomy for Sustainable Finance

🟡 Second Reading of the Scams (Countermeasures) and Other Matters Bill — Wrap-Up Speech 🇸🇬

Item ID: item-20260909-daily-exploratory-search-9fdc3e64ee
⏰ Deadline: October 2026 (exact date not stated)
Products: Deposit accounts, payments

Singapore’s Ministry of Home Affairs described proposed Account Disabling, Disclosure and Service Limitation Orders in a Second Reading wrap-up speech, including information exchange with service providers and obligations imposed by those orders. It said local banks will offer an LPBA or alternative basic account from October 2026 to affected Singapore citizens or permanent residents except in limited cases, without stating an exact date. This is a legislative-stage government speech, not an enacted rule or MAS instrument, and may inform monitoring of the operational and customer-access trade-off in scam-related account restrictions without creating a Hong Kong obligation.

Source: Second Reading of the Scams (Countermeasures) and Other Matters Bill — Wrap-Up Speech
