Hong Kong


🟡 Review of the Enhanced Competency Framework for Banking Practitioners 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260923-hkma-brdr-20260922-1-en
⏰ Deadline: Indicative only — new ECF guidance and transition arrangements by mid-2028; new courses envisaged in 2030
Products: Lending, Investments

HKMA’s 23 September circular to all AIs proposes a common Core Level, ten Professional-Level workstreams, revised experience requirements, certification retention across role changes and integrated qualifications. It advises AIs to keep current ECF training uninterrupted while HKMA and HKIB develop guidance and transition arrangements by mid-2028; updated content and new courses are envisaged in 2030. The circular is a proposed change rather than a replacement framework or fixed compliance date, but workforce inventory, role mobility, qualification pathways and training capacity may need early transition planning. Detailed competency standards, exemptions and transition treatment remain to be set in later guidance.

Source: Review of the Enhanced Competency Framework for Banking Practitioners


🟡 SFC unveils Strategic Action Plan to support Hong Kong’s First Five-Year Plan and Policy Address in promoting capital market development 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260923-sfc-web-26pr148
⏰ Deadline: 2027: planned Swap Connect reference rate; planned consultation on prospectus-disclosure streamlining
Products: Investments, FX/RMB, Digital assets

The SFC announced its Strategic Action Plan on 23 September, setting out short-, medium- and long-term measures across RMB internationalisation, mutual access, wealth management, market quality and financial security. Listed initiatives include an RMB trading counter under southbound Stock Connect, product-suite expansion, T+1 settlement, a Swap Connect reference rate within 2027, responsible AI, and digital-asset licensing, custody and surveillance capabilities. The plan signals initiatives and future consultation rather than a binding rule or immediate all-AI obligation; applicability to banks depends on their SFC-regulated entities or activities. Securities, FX/RMB, digital-asset and cross-border teams may need coordinated horizon scanning of licences, products, systems and Mainland dependencies as subsequent instruments emerge.

Source: SFC unveils Strategic Action Plan to support Hong Kong’s First Five-Year Plan and Policy Address in promoting capital market development

🟡 Firms crack down on money mules but need to do more 🇬🇧
Item ID: item-20260923-global-material-incidents-enforcement-fca-money-mules
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Deposit accounts, Payments, Digital channels

The FCA’s 23 September release reports a completed review of 35 UK retail banks, building societies, challenger banks, payment institutions and e-money institutions, rather than a new rule or enforcement action. It reports that 238,396 suspected mule accounts were closed in 2025 and that fraudulent funds commonly cashed out through the second to fifth account; some accounts showed repeated suspected mule activity before closure. The FCA cautions that more closures may reflect customer growth and improved identification rather than a higher mule proportion, and says it is working with industry and law enforcement on intelligence sharing. This is an analogous UK benchmark only: comparable Hong Kong deposit-account and payment journeys may consider network detection, early intervention and lawful sharing controls, without inferring local prevalence or a Hong Kong legal basis for sharing.

Source: Firms crack down on money mules but need to do more
