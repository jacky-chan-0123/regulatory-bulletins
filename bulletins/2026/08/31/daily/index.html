🟡 Early Alert on United Nations Sanctions 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260828-sfc-aml-circulars-26EC53
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Investment, digital, virtual-asset services

The SFC published AML/CFT circular 26EC53, “Early Alert on United Nations Sanctions”, on 28 August 2026 for licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities. The official metadata supports a sanctions-related alert, but the available run evidence does not establish a new rule, screening update, rescreening, reporting obligation, deadline or applicability to authorised institutions generally. It may affect a bank group only where an SFC-regulated entity, licensed virtual-asset service provider or shared financial-crime screening service falls within the final scope. Financial Crime Compliance should obtain the signed circular and attachments, confirm applicability, and make only source-supported changes while retaining list-version, configuration, reconciliation and case evidence.

Source: SFC circular 26EC53 — Early Alert on United Nations Sanctions


🟡 BEA Signs Memorandum of Understanding with Anchorpoint to Explore Real-World HKD-backed Stablecoin Applications 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260828-hk-banks-news-20260828en-1
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Payments, digital, cross-border GBA

BEA and Anchorpoint announced a memorandum of understanding on 28 August 2026 to explore digital-asset initiatives using HKDAP, described in the joint announcement as a regulated HKD-backed stablecoin. The parties say they will explore secure and compliant customer solutions and practical payment and settlement use cases, with further arrangement details to follow. This is an exploration partnership, not a launched customer product, regulatory action or Hong Kong requirement. A comparable initiative may affect payment, settlement and cross-border product strategy, and would need decisions on applicability, customer and entity scope, end-to-end flows, controls and evidence before implementation.

Source: BEA and Anchorpoint joint announcement
Primary source: official document
