Executive Summary

Hong Kong


🟡 SFC early alert on UN sanctions-list amendments 🇭🇰

Item ID: item-20260814-sfc-aml-circulars-4a43312779
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Investment services, payments, virtual assets

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) issued circular SFO/IS/030/2026 on 14 August after the relevant UN Security Council committee amended details for three individuals and one entity on the ISIL (Da’esh) and Al-Qaida sanctions list on 13 August, New York time. The SFC expects licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual asset service providers and associated entities to update customer and payment screening databases; it presents this within the expected standards in its 7 February 2018 UN-sanctions circular, not as a new bank-wide rule. The direct perimeter is the named SFC-regulated entities, with bank relevance where registered securities activity or shared screening is involved; delayed data ingestion or incomplete screening may affect client, beneficial-owner and counterparty screening. The SFC says an updated Cap. 537CB list was published on 14 August, while the corresponding Cap. 575 Gazette update was pending; it states no screening population, system design or compliance deadline.

Source: SFC circular: Early Alert on United Nations Sanctions
