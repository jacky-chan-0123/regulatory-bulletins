Hong Kong


🟡 First cohort of GenA.I. Sandbox++ 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260827-hkma-press-74e83c55f1
⏰ Deadline: Technical trials commence later in 2026; no specific date stated.
Products: Digital banking; SME onboarding; Payments

The HKMA, SFC, IA and MPFA announced the first GenA.I. Sandbox++ cohort: 36 selected use cases from nearly 100 proposals, involving 30 financial institutions and 27 technology partners. The controlled testing covers agentic-AI applications across end-to-end customer onboarding, payments, insurance claims and customer interactions, with Cyberport’s platform hosting technical trials later in 2026. This is a testing initiative with early supervisory engagement, not a rule, direction or industry-wide obligation; the release does not identify participating authorised institutions or specify control standards, approval requirements or implementation dates. For participating or comparable planned digital journeys, a proportionate commercial and risk preparation step is to identify applicability and consider governance, human oversight, customer communications, data flows, vendor diligence, testing and monitoring evidence.
