Hong Kong


🟡 Preferential carried-interest regime: FSTB clarifies proposed scope 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260812-fstb-4127eae687
⏰ Deadline: Subject to Legislative Council passage, proposed application from year of assessment 2025/26; Government targets resumption of second reading in H2 2026.
Products: Investment; wealth management; marketing

FSTB says the Bill proposes to extend eligible carried interest from private equity to profits of other eligible funds, while proprietary trading using own capital remains outside the stated fund definition. It states that eligible carried interest must be performance-linked and non-discretionary, earned by a manager or qualifying employee for Hong Kong investment-management services, and subject to other conditions; proposed refinements include associates and receipt through other entities. This is a policy clarification on a Bill, not enacted legislation or IRD guidance, so it does not establish a current requirement for any bank entity or product. If enacted, it may affect fund-management, wealth/investment distribution, remuneration arrangements and related client communications; Tax, Wealth/Asset Management, Legal and Compliance can retain a conditional applicability assessment until enacted text and IRD guidance clarify eligibility evidence and transition.

Source: FSTB responds to media enquiries regarding preferential tax regime for carried interest

Mainland/GBA


🟡 PBOC 15th Five-Year Plan signals cross-border financial priorities 🇨🇳
Item ID: item-20260810-gba-official-6a2d24667d
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Payments; FX; cross-border GBA

PBOC announced that it had issued its reform-and-development plan for the 15th Five-Year Plan period and nine supporting action plans. The release signals deeper two-way financial-market opening, stronger cross-border financial-infrastructure connectivity, offshore RMB market development, a broadly accessible cross-border RMB payment system, and an elevated role for Hong Kong as an international financial centre; it also names e-CNY, credit information and stronger AML oversight. This is a Mainland policy-plan announcement rather than evidence of a new Hong Kong legal or supervisory obligation, and it states no commencement, transition, reporting deadline or addressee-specific requirement. Hong Kong entities supporting Mainland/GBA SME trade, RMB payments, FX, custody or market access may face future infrastructure, data/AML and partner dependencies; relevant business and Compliance teams can monitor authoritative Chinese text and the supporting action plans before assessing any customer-journey, control or provider changes.

Source: The PBOC Releases the PBOC Reform and Development Plan for the 15th Five-Year Plan Period

🟡 UK T+1 readiness: FCA signals more intrusive supervision 🇬🇧
Item ID: item-20260813-fca-benchmark-ef790d1388
⏰ Deadline: UK critical Taskforce recommendations: end-December 2026; UK T+1 settlement transition: 11 October 2027.
Products: Investments; UK securities settlement; custody

The FCA says the UK will move to T+1 securities settlement on 11 October 2027 and expects participants to be well into implementation and finalising testing; it may act where firms are inadequately prepared. It says many critical Accelerated Settlement Taskforce recommendations are expected by end-December 2026, including trade-date allocation/confirmation and the FMSB standard for sharing settlement instructions. This is UK supervisory commentary and Taskforce material, not a Hong Kong requirement and not itself a UK rulebook obligation. For entities participating in the UK securities settlement chain, the comparable operational benchmark is to identify client, custodian, broker and outsourced-provider dependencies and preserve evidence of allocation, confirmation, settlement-instruction, testing and settlement-failure monitoring readiness.

Primary source: official document
Source: T+1 Settlement: are firms ready for 2027?
