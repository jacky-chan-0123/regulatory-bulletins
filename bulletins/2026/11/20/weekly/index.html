Hong Kong


🔴 Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261119-hkma-circulars-ef9980b664
⏰ Deadline: End of Q3 2027 (compliance-status report to HKMA)
Products: SME onboarding, lending, digital

HKMA expects all authorised institutions to review the HKAB Guideline on Corruption Prevention for Banks, assess existing policies and procedures, plan necessary enhancements where appropriate, and report compliance status by end-Q3 2027. The Guideline consolidates existing regulations and statutory requirements with practical measures and good practices covering governance, whistleblowing, operational controls, consequence management and training. This is a supervisory expectation rather than a new statutory rule or a prescribed uniform control design. The most immediate management issue is whether anti-corruption control evidence is complete across onboarding, lending, payments, procurement and third-party activity, staff escalation and training.

Actions:
• Internal planning recommendation (not an HKMA deadline): within 30 days, assign accountable first-line and Compliance ownership for an applicability and gap assessment; have second line challenge scope and the reporting plan.
• Before the report is due, remediate proportionate gaps and retain policy, training, whistleblowing, testing, case-log and closure evidence.

Source: Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks

🟡 UK financial watchdogs clarify cyber expectations as attacks rise 🇬🇧
Item ID: item-20261119-global-material-incidents-enforcement-b8bc1c49f2
⏰ Deadline: 26 February 2027 (UK consultation response deadline; no Hong Kong deadline stated)
Products: Digital, payments

The FCA, PRA and Bank of England propose guidance that consolidates existing UK requirements and supervisory lessons on cyber risk and resilience. The proposed guidance addresses governance and identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to and recovering from cyber incidents, while remaining deliberately non-rigid. Consultation responses to GC26/9 close on 26 February 2027, and final guidance is expected in the first half of 2027. It is an analogous benchmark for Hong Kong digital and payment services, particularly where a UK-group perimeter or shared service or vendor dependency is material; it does not establish a Hong Kong control gap.

Source: UK financial watchdogs clarify cyber expectations as attacks rise
