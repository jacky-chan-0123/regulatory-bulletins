Hong Kong


🔴 Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261119-hkma-brdr-ef9980b664
⏰ Deadline: End of Q3 2027 (compliance-status report to HKMA)
Products: Bank-wide operations

HKMA’s circular to all authorized institutions introduces HKAB’s Guideline on Corruption Prevention for Banks. HKMA expects institutions to assess their practices against the guideline, make necessary enhancements where appropriate, and report compliance status by end-Q3 2027. The circular describes the guideline as a consolidated reference that reinforces existing regulations and statutory requirements; its good practices cover governance, whistleblowing, operational controls, consequence management and training.

Actions:
• Within 30 days, assign accountable first-line and Compliance owners for an applicability and gap assessment, with second-line challenge.
• Map relevant controls and evidence to the guideline, including risk assessment, policy, training, whistleblowing, testing, case-log and closure evidence.
• Establish reporting ownership and sequence proportionate remediation ahead of the end-Q3 2027 status report.

Source: Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks

🟡 GC26/9: Cyber risk and resilience management 🇬🇧
Item ID: item-20261119-global-material-incidents-enforcement-e994140b4f
⏰ Deadline: 26 February 2027 (consultation response deadline)
Products: Digital services; Payments

The FCA, PRA and Bank of England opened a joint consultation on non-Handbook guidance that clarifies existing UK rules rather than adding rules. The draft expects proportionate accountability, inventories of assets and third-party dependencies, layered access controls, detection, and tested response and recovery for specified UK firms. It is not a Hong Kong requirement; any relevance to Hong Kong digital or payment services is analogous and depends on UK-perimeter entities or shared-service dependencies.

Source: GC26/9: Cyber risk and resilience management
