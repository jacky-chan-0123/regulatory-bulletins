Hong Kong Regulatory Developments


🟡 Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) (Amendment) Rules 2026 and Securities and Futures Ordinance (Amendment of Schedule 8) Order 2026 🇭🇰

Item ID: item-20261125-fstb-stock-listing-rules
⏰ Deadline: 1 March 2027 (stated commencement date)
Products: Listed securities, investment services

The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau and Securities and Futures Commission state that the Amendment Rules and the Schedule 8 Order are scheduled for Gazette publication on 27 November, LegCo tabling on 2 December and commencement on 1 March 2027. The changes will give the SFC powers to impose continuing and post-listing conditions, require information from listed issuers, and amend, revoke or impose conditions; non-compliance with a post-listing condition may lead to an SFC direction to suspend dealings. The Schedule 8 changes extend Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal review to specified decisions under the amended rules. Listed bank groups and securities businesses supporting listing applicants may need to confirm affected entities and prepare condition-management, escalation and SFC-information response workflows; the brief does not establish a current duty before commencement or a bank-specific perimeter.

Source: Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) (Amendment) Rules 2026 and Securities and Futures Ordinance (Amendment of Schedule 8) Order 2026


🟡 Speech by SFST in moving the Second Reading of Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026 🇭🇰

Item ID: item-20261125-fstb-mpf-speech
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: MPF administration, retirement investment, employer contributions

The Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury moved the Second Reading of the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026 on 25 November, so the measures described remain proposals rather than enacted obligations. The speech proposes a two-tier surcharge for overdue mandatory contributions, including a 10% surcharge where arrears exceed 120 days, and a power for the MPFA to require suspected defaulting employers to provide employee-income information within a specified period. It also proposes trustee fines and/or public reprimands that cannot be passed to schemes, constituent funds or members, disciplinary action for MPF-intermediary misconduct, and investment-rule flexibility. Bank-affiliated MPF trustees or intermediaries, and group employers, may need to monitor the Bill and conditionally map arrears, notice-data, disciplinary-governance and member-charge processes; no commencement or compliance date is stated.

Source: Speech by SFST in moving the Second Reading of Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026


🟡 Launch of CMU Digital Asset Platform 🇭🇰

Item ID: item-20261125-hkma-press-5c7fd95a47
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Debt securities, settlement, digital assets

The HKMA has launched the CMU Digital Asset Platform, a distributed-ledger platform operated by CMU OmniClear on its behalf. It supports paperless debt-security issuance, secondary clearing and settlement, coupon payments, redemption and tokenisation, with atomic on-chain delivery-versus-payment, RTGS and CBDC interfaces, and 24-hour multi-currency settlement using cash tokens. The stated roadmap explores funds, real-world assets, tokenised deposits and regulated stablecoins, which are not described as current services. This is not a participation mandate or new authorized-institution obligation; debt-market participants considering use may need to assess settlement, resilience, data, legal-perimeter and customer or product dependencies before adoption.

Source: Launch of CMU Digital Asset Platform
