Hong Kong


🟡 Government welcomes passage of Banking Legislation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Bill 2026 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261118-hkma-press-ee865a8bb3
⏰ Deadline: Phased commencement starts 1 January 2027; later phases are not stated
Products: Deposits, Lending, Payments

The Government announced Legislative Council passage of the Banking Legislation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Bill 2026, now the Banking Legislation (Miscellaneous Amendments) Ordinance 2026. The Ordinance amends the Banking Ordinance on bank-holding-company regulation, skilled-person engagement for supervisory purposes, enforcement modernisation, the move from a three-tier to a two-tier banking system, and technical matters; it also amends the HKAB and Financial Institutions (Resolution) Ordinances. It will commence in phases from 1 January 2027. Authorised institutions, relevant holding companies and resolution-regime participants may need to assess structural, authorisation-perimeter, enforcement-governance and operating-model effects, while the release does not state provision-specific transition duties or later commencement dates.

🟡 MAS and ABS Set Out PayNow Generation 2 Implementation Roadmap 🇸🇬
Item ID: item-20261118-mas-regulatory-announcements-paynow-g2
⏰ Deadline: Singapore roadmap: end-2027 foundational standards and trials; staged 2028 rollouts
Products: Payments, Digital, Merchant payments

MAS and the Association of Banks in Singapore published a PayNow Gen2 implementation roadmap for Singapore. The roadmap covers seven staged enhancements, including SGQR+ interoperability, deep-linking, selected higher-value government payments, person-to-merchant dispute, fraud and scam arrangements, richer payment data and micropayment testing. Common deep-linking standards and a central facility are targeted by end-2027, with limited SGQR+ and higher-value-payment trials planned in 2027 and staged expansion in 2028. For comparable Hong Kong payment providers, the roadmap may inform monitoring of deep-link security, QR routing, dispute evidence and data, reconciliation, and cross-border interoperability; it is not an immediate MAS rule and does not establish Hong Kong requirements.

Source: MAS and ABS Set Out PayNow Generation 2 Implementation Roadmap
