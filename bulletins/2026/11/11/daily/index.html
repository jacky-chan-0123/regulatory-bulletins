Executive Summary

• 🟡 HKMA proposes a capital-treatment change for specified Northern Metropolis-related entities; final legislative scope and timing will determine any effect on project-lending capital economics.

Hong Kong


🟡 Northern Metropolis Financial Advisory Taskforce holds its third meeting 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261110-hkma-press-922a99cc87
⏰ Deadline: HKMA intends to submit proposed amendments to Legislative Council in Q1 2027, with a view to effect in Q2 2027
Products: Project lending

HKMA proposes amending the Banking (Capital) Rules to designate government-related entities, including 11 post-secondary institutions and three park companies, as Domestic Public Sector Entities. It states that the measure would lower banks’ capital costs and help those entities obtain bank financing for Northern Metropolis projects. HKMA intends to submit the proposed amendments to Legislative Council in Q1 2027, with a view to their taking effect in Q2 2027. This is a proposal and stated legislative timetable, not an enacted rule or current capital-treatment requirement; for potentially affected lenders, final entity and facility scope may affect project-lending capital economics, credit appetite and pricing.

Source: Northern Metropolis Financial Advisory Taskforce holds its third meeting
