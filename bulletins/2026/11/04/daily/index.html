🟡 Future of Finance Institute Commences Operations, Starting with AI in Finance 🇸🇬
Item ID: item-20261103-daily-exploratory-search-mas-ffi
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Digital banking, AI-enabled services

MAS announced on 3 November that Singapore’s public-private Future of Finance Institute (FFI) has commenced operations. Its initial work on AI in finance comprises PathFin.ai, BuildFin.ai and SafeFin.ai, including experience sharing and good practices for deploying agentic AI. MAS says the work will cover practical resources for its AI Risk Management Guidelines, including AI use-case risk assessment, testing and assurance, and third-party AI. This is an industry initiative rather than a new binding instrument or Hong Kong requirement, but may serve as a regional benchmark for AI governance and provider oversight where Singapore operations or shared AI services are material.
