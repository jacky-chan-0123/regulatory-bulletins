Hong Kong


🟡 HSBC RedCoin surpasses 100,000 users 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261126-hk-banks-news-ccf9e83f3a
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Payments, digital wallet, tokenised investments

HSBC says its Hong Kong dollar-denominated stablecoin, RedCoin (RCHKD), has surpassed 100,000 users approximately one month after launch. It says customers can use RedCoin through PayMe for instant transfers and payments at more than 100,000 merchants, while eligible customers can access a tokenised unit class of an established HKD money-market fund through its mobile-banking app. HSBC says broader wealth-management and wholesale use cases will proceed in stages, reflecting client needs, technology readiness and regulatory expectations. This is an official bank product-adoption update rather than an authority action; it may affect comparable payment, wallet, investment-distribution and associated customer-protection, financial-crime and resilience considerations, subject to each institution’s product and customer perimeter.
