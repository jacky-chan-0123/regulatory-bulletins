Executive Summary

• 16 November effective date: HKSCC participant changes for the Uncertificated Securities Market introduce USI transfer checks, six-year record retention and revised fee flows for affected roles.
• Crypto-asset reporting framework enacted: the relevant ordinance was gazetted on 13 November, but the retrieved record does not establish commencement, scope or operational reporting duties.

Hong Kong


🟡 Amendments to HKSCC Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI Terms and Conditions 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261109-daily-exploratory-search-b7a6f2ee17
⏰ Deadline: 16 November 2026
Products: Investment, securities settlement

HKSCC states that SFC-approved amendments to its Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI terms take effect on 16 November 2026 in support of the Uncertificated Securities Market and revised fees. For participating securities, only Stamp Authorized Persons may submit USI transfer requests, and they must perform specified identity and beneficial-interest checks and retain supporting records for at least six years. The circular also provides for ASR fees payable by non-registrar participants, including through EPI. Affected CCASS participants, Stamp Authorized Persons, Investor Participants and outsourced securities-operations providers should determine role and security scope, then prepare the related workflow, evidence-retention and fee-reconciliation changes.

Source: Amendments to HKSCC Rules, Operational Procedures and FINI Terms and Conditions


🟡 Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and amended CRS ordinance gazetted 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261113-legco-crypto-asset-reporting-framework
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Digital assets, investment

The LegCo record states that the Inland Revenue amendment Bill passed on 4 November 2026, with Administration amendments, and that the resulting ordinance was gazetted on 13 November 2026. The retrieved record establishes enactment and gazettal only; it does not provide the enacted text, commencement, in-scope reporting entities, due-diligence steps, reporting fields, thresholds, exemptions or transition. Entities offering crypto-asset services or holding potentially reportable customer and transaction data may need tax-reporting, onboarding, data-lineage, reporting and vendor assessment once the operative instrument is confirmed. Tax, Legal and Digital Assets should obtain the gazetted ordinance and commencement provisions before assigning implementation.

Source: Inland Revenue (Amendment) (Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and Amended Common Reporting Standard) Bill 2026


🟡 Second Reading moved for Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2026 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261111-fstb-securities-futures-amendment-bill
⏰ Deadline: Conditional on passage: 4 December 2027 expected for specified changes
Products: Investment, REITs

On 11 November, the Financial Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury moved the Bill’s Second Reading. The Bill would introduce statutory REIT restructuring and compulsory-acquisition mechanisms, and clarify market-misconduct and disclosure coverage for exchange-listed collective investment schemes. If passed, the REIT and market-misconduct amendments would commence on gazettal, while specified inside-information and interests changes are expected on 4 December 2027 to allow familiarisation with revised electronic forms and guidance. This is a Bill rather than enacted law or a final SFC rule, so investment businesses should take a conditional applicability inventory rather than treat the proposals as current obligations.

Source: Second Reading moved for Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2026


🟡 Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026 gazetted 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261113-fstb-mpf-amendment-bill
⏰ Deadline: 25 November 2026 — First Reading, not a compliance deadline
Products: MPF, retirement investment

The Government gazetted the Bill on 13 November for First Reading on 25 November. It proposes a second-tier 10% surcharge where MPF arrears, including the existing 5% surcharge, remain unpaid 120 days after contribution day, alongside MPFA information notices with at least 14 days to respond. It also proposes trustee sanctions of up to HK$10 million or three times profit gained or loss avoided, whichever is greater, public reprimand, a prohibition on passing trustee penalties to schemes, funds or members, and intermediary discipline and investment/risk-management measures. The proposals are not current obligations; MPF trustees, intermediaries and group employers should assess role applicability and preserve a conditional mapping of arrears, notice, governance and member-charge processes pending final legislation and MPFA guidance.

Source: Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment) Bill 2026 gazetted


🟡 HKMA and SFC co-host tokenisation seminar under Project Ensemble 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261112-hkma-press-2751460376-project-ensemble-tokenisation-seminar
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Tokenised deposits, payments, investments

HKMA and SFC co-hosted an 11 November seminar on Project Ensemble and tokenised SFC-authorised investment products. The 12 November release says Ensemble TX enables 24/7 interbank settlement of tokenised deposits and refers to SFC approval of round-the-clock secondary trading for tokenised money-market funds. HKMA says it will work with SFC and industry to broaden tokenised-deposit settlement of tokenised investment products. This is a market-development announcement rather than a rule, consultation, control prescription or participation requirement; institutions considering relevant products may assess strategic, settlement, custody, customer-disclosure and perimeter dependencies before a product decision.

Source: HKMA and SFC co-host seminar to advance tokenisation in Hong Kong and promote collaborative opportunities within Project Ensemble
