Hong Kong


🟡 SFST moves Second Reading of Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2026 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261111-fstb-sfo-amendment-bill
⏰ Deadline: Conditional on passage: certain disclosure amendments are expected on 4 December 2027; other stated amendments would take effect on gazettal after passage.
Products: Listed REITs, listed collective investment schemes

On 11 November, the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury moved Second Reading of the Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2026. The Bill proposes statutory mechanisms for REIT restructurings and compulsory acquisitions, and would clarify the application of market-misconduct and disclosure provisions to exchange-listed collective investment schemes. If passed, the REIT and market-misconduct changes would apply on gazettal, while amendments concerning inside-information and interests disclosure are expected on 4 December 2027 to allow time for revised electronic forms and guidance. This is a legislative proposal rather than enacted law or a final SFC rule; the source does not provide final thresholds, affected roles or transition detail. Banks supporting Hong Kong-listed REITs or listed collective investment schemes may need a conditional applicability assessment across conduct surveillance, disclosure processes, product governance and customer communications once final text and guidance are available.

Source: LegCo: SFST moves Second Reading of the Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2026


🟡 LCQ14: Building international gold trading market 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261111-fstb-lcq14
⏰ Deadline: Planned official launch in Q1 2027; no source-stated bank compliance deadline.
Products: OTC gold, clearing and settlement

The government says Hong Kong’s central clearing and settlement system for gold began trial operation in July 2026, with 11 banks as provisional direct participants for over-the-counter gold transactions. The system is planned to launch officially in Q1 2027 and, before launch, is to connect with RTGS to enable delivery-versus-payment settlement. FSTB is also exploring co-operation with the London Bullion Market Association on delivery standards, while HKMA is taking forward plans for moderate Exchange Fund gold increases and local spot- and futures-market participation. This is a policy and infrastructure update, not a participation rule: the source does not state final admission criteria, operating rules, delivery standards, RTGS timing or a bank deadline. Prospective participants may need to prepare decisions on bullion settlement, custody, liquidity and operational connectivity once participant terms are issued; non-participants have no source-backed change obligation.

Source: LCQ14: Building international gold trading market
