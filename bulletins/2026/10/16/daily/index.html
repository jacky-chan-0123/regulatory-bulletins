Executive Summary

• 🔴 HKMA has issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions, replacing its 2019 AI principles and expecting proportionate lifecycle controls to be uplifted in a timely manner.
• Priority decision: establish accountable ownership and a risk/materiality inventory for deployed and planned AI use cases, focusing first on customer-impacting, autonomous and third-party-enabled use.
• No fixed implementation deadline is stated. The accompanying Sandbox report offers non-exhaustive practical insights, while the agentic-AI concept note is preliminary and does not create current requirements.

Hong Kong


🔴 Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261015-hkma-brdr-d0822635c9
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Digital banking, SME onboarding

HKMA’s 15 October circular to all Authorized Institutions introduces its Accountability, Resilience and Trust (ART) framework, and Annex A supersedes the 2019 high-level AI principles. HKMA expects institutions to apply the guidance proportionately, with board and senior-management accountability, a strategy, named use-case owners, an up-to-date inventory, and risk-based lifecycle governance. For material or higher-risk use cases, the guidance describes more stringent review, including senior-management sign-off and oversight, independent validation where appropriate, and controls for monitoring, recovery, records, data, cyber and third-party risks. The customer-facing implications include output quality, transparency, fair treatment and review or reassessment where outputs have actual consequences; HKMA says policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner but states no fixed date.

Actions:
• Immediately assign accountable owners to deployed AI use cases and complete an applicability and materiality triage, prioritising customer-facing, autonomous and vendor-enabled uses.
• In the near term, map higher-risk uses to ART expectations for approvals, testing, monitoring, data and supplier safeguards, fallback/recovery arrangements, and auditable evidence; obtain proportionate independent challenge.
• Treat Annex B as implementation insight rather than a new requirement, and monitor the forthcoming discussion paper because Annex C is preliminary and does not create current agentic-AI requirements.

Source: Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence
