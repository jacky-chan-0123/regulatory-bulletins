Hong Kong


🟡 SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261012-sfc-web-26pr166
⏰ Deadline: 16 November 2026; existing issuers must participate within five years
Products: Investment, Custody

The SFC approved six companies as approved securities registrars with effect from 16 November 2026, when the USM regime takes effect. It identifies Computershare Hong Kong Investor Services Limited and Tricor Investor Services Limited as the first approved for USM-related services. Listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar, while post-16 November listing applicants with uncertificated securities must appoint one approved for USM-related services. A listed group entity, custodian or investment-services business may need to assess registrar appointments, register and settlement dependencies, and client-servicing implications; the announcement does not create a direct duty for every bank.

🟡 SFC proceeds to strengthen targeted tools to tackle IPO-related and other corporate misconduct 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261012-sfc-web-26pr165
⏰ Deadline: Q1 2027 expected commencement, subject to legislative process
Products: Investment, IPO advisory

Following consultation, the SFC will proceed with clarified and refined amendments to the Stock Market Listing Rules and related Securities and Futures Ordinance provisions. The proposed changes include continuing conditions on listing applicants and post-listing conditions as alternatives to immediate suspension, streamlined resumption procedures, and a merits-review mechanism through the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal. The SFC expects commencement in the first quarter of 2027, subject to gazetting, negative vetting and the legislative process. This is an announced rule-change process rather than a current final obligation; SFC-registered corporate-finance businesses and group entities supporting listings may need to monitor final provisions and assess case-governance, client-advice and escalation implications.
