🟡 Building the future of finance in the age of AI 🇬🇧

Item ID: item-20261019-global-material-incidents-enforcement-326b1a180b
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Digital banking, Payments, Investments

In a drafted speech delivered on 19 October, the Financial Conduct Authority described AI good and poor practice drawn from firm testing, including human escalation for chatbot vulnerability signals and shortcomings in testing, post-deployment governance and static monitoring. It highlighted clear accountability, risk-based controls, human oversight, escalation triggers, stress testing, fallback options and understanding model-supplier concentration and dependencies; it also said significant AI failures and near misses should be covered by UK major-incident reporting. For comparable Hong Kong digital servicing, payment or investment journeys, this is an analogous control benchmark rather than a Hong Kong obligation; management may identify customer-impacting or agentic AI use cases and test whether escalation, fallback, incident and supplier-dependency evidence is available.
