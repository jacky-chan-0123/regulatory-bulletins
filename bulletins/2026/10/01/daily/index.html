Executive Summary

• The SFC has set a conditional Q2 2028 implementation programme for HKIDR-DM; in-scope securities businesses should sequence client-data, consent and order-management changes against the published preparation milestones.
• Individual clients without express HKIDR-DM consent will be unable to open exchange-traded derivatives positions once the regime is implemented, creating a material customer-journey and revenue dependency for relevant intermediaries.
• HKMA reports that three participating banks have approved seven IP-financing Sandbox pilots, providing a voluntary SME-lending market-development reference rather than a new underwriting requirement.
• HSBC has named its planned Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin and describes a phased payments launch in H2 2026; the announcement does not verify licence terms, launch date or corporate availability.

Hong Kong


🟡 Roadmap for Implementing the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the Exchange-traded Derivatives Market 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260930-sfc-circulars-26ec61
⏰ Deadline: Preparation from Q3 2026; Q2 2028 scheduled launch subject to readiness
Products: Listed derivatives, securities operations, client onboarding

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) signals a future HKIDR-DM implementation programme for relevant licensed corporations and registered institutions, with a Q2 2028 launch scheduled subject to market readiness, testing and rehearsals. Its roadmap sets preparation periods for consent and CID updates through Q4 2027, OMS enhancement to Q3 2027, and BCAN/mapping-file preparation, testing and submission through Q1 2028. The proposed Code provision will be gazetted and take effect on a future SFC-determined date, so the roadmap does not fix commencement or replace future HKEX technical requirements. In-scope businesses may need coordinated client-data, OMS, broker-chain, data-lineage and testing work; corporate CID is in scope, while the individual-consent requirement does not apply to corporate clients.

Source: Roadmap for Implementing the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the Exchange-traded Derivatives Market


🟡 Obtaining client consent under the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the exchange-traded derivatives market (HKIDR-DM) 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260930-sfc-circulars-26ec60
⏰ Deadline: Q2 2028 scheduled launch; Code effective date to be determined
Products: Listed derivatives, client onboarding, securities operations

The SFC sets out forthcoming supervisory expectations for relevant intermediaries to obtain express consent from individual clients for specified CID and BCAN collection and transfer under HKIDR-DM. Consent cannot be inferred; without it, an intermediary may not enter an order or trade that opens a position, although it may enter an order or trade that closes an existing listed-derivatives position. The circular calls for identity authentication, retained consent and withdrawal records, and a written log retained during the client relationship and for at least two years afterwards. Paragraph 5.6A is still to be gazetted and will take effect on an SFC-determined date, so the stated Q2 2028 schedule is not a current legal effective date and the individual-consent requirement does not extend to corporate clients.

Source: Obtaining client consent under the Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime for the exchange-traded derivatives market (HKIDR-DM)


🟡 Participating banks in IP Financing Sandbox complete credit approval for first batch of pilot cases 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260930-hkma-press-a41ebf3faf
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: SME lending, IP-backed finance

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) reports that three participating banks completed credit approvals for seven IP Financing Sandbox pilots, with loans from HK$1 million to HK$39 million for working capital, expansion and market promotion. The Sandbox, launched in December 2025 with government bodies and supported by HKAB, uses independent IP valuation reports in credit assessment and is described as a risk-controlled pilot. This is an implementation option and voluntary product-development reference, not a new supervisory obligation, mandated valuation standard or participation requirement. Banks considering comparable IP-backed SME lending may need to assess credit policy, valuation independence, collateral enforceability, fraud and conflict risk, customer disclosure, and third-party valuation capability; pilot outcomes and operating effectiveness are not stated.

Source: Participating banks in IP Financing Sandbox complete credit approval for first batch of pilot cases


🟡 HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’ 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260930-hk-banks-news-6523e8b396
⏰ Deadline: H2 2026 planned launch (bank statement; no confirmed date)
Products: Payments, digital assets, mobile app

HSBC says its forthcoming Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin will be called HSBC RedCoin and that it plans a second-half 2026 launch under a licence it says was granted in April. At launch, it says availability will be limited to PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App, initially for P2P and P2M payments before prospective wholesale corporate and institutional use cases. It also says it will publish scam-prevention and redemption education. This is a bank market announcement and commercial development, not an HKMA or SFC instrument: it does not verify licence terms, a launch date, corporate availability, redemption design, reserve arrangements or cross-border scope.

Source: HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’
