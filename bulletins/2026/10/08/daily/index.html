🔴 Consultation Conclusions on Proposed Amendments to the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261007-sfc-consultations-25cc10
⏰ Deadline: Tentative effective date: 1 November 2026 following gazettal; existing schemes generally have 12 months from the effective date for liquidity, MMF and offering-document changes
Products: Authorised funds, MMFs, investment distribution

The SFC says it will adopt amended Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds Code proposals, tentatively effective on 1 November 2026 following gazettal. New schemes must comply from the effective date, while existing schemes generally receive 12 months for liquidity, MMF and offering-document changes. The changes cover an alternative VaR approach, anti-dilution tools for certain daily-dealing funds investing mainly in less-liquid assets, MMF liquidity and credit provisions, CNAV safeguards, and KFS classification disclosure. The direct scope is SFC-authorised schemes and their management companies; bank distributors, trustees and custodians need role-specific analysis, and supplementary guidance, FAQs and templates remain to be issued.

Actions: Establish an entity-and-fund applicability inventory, accountable owner and transition plan now; map ADT, liquidity, MMF/CNAV, VaR and KFS controls to the final text. Retain configuration, validation, disclosure, testing and customer-communication evidence, and escalate any gazettal or vendor dependency that could affect transition timing.

Source: Consultation Conclusions on Proposed Amendments to the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds


🟡 LCQ17: Accelerated settlement for cash market 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261007-daily-exploratory-search-db1220fb15
⏰ Deadline: HKEX targets consultation conclusions and implementation arrangements within 2026; no final implementation date stated
Products: Securities settlement, custody, investment services

The Government’s Legislative Council reply says HKEX is preparing with the market for a proposed T+1 cash-market settlement cycle, subject to co-ordination with relevant Mainland units. HKEX targets consultation conclusions and implementation arrangements within 2026 and is considering revised clearing and settlement timetables, longer settlement-instruction hours, allocation and confirmation tools, and a stakeholder workflow platform. It says specifications and guidance will follow, with preparation and testing time; the reply does not confirm an implementation date or participant requirements. Banks acting as brokers, custodians, fund managers or settlement participants may be affected through post-trade cut-offs, funding, client confirmation, systems and vendor dependencies.

Source: LCQ17: Accelerated settlement for cash market


🟡 SFST articulates Hong Kong's gold master plan at Global Precious Metals Conference 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261007-fstb-gold-master-plan
⏰ Deadline: Formal launch of the Gold Central Clearing and Settlement System is scheduled for Q1 2027
Products: Bullion settlement, RTGS, cross-border markets

FSTB reports that the trial Hong Kong Gold Central Clearing and Settlement System has 11 banks as provisional direct participants, with formal launch scheduled for Q1 2027. Connection to Hong Kong RTGS for delivery-versus-payment settlement is in its final stages. The Government and the London Bullion Market Association are exploring delivery-standards co-operation, contingent on materialisation, and tax concessions are being explored. This is a policy and market-infrastructure update, not a mandatory participation requirement or final rulebook; provisional or prospective participants may need to consider clearing, RTGS, custody and settlement-risk dependencies.

Source: SFST articulates Hong Kong's gold master plan at Global Precious Metals Conference
