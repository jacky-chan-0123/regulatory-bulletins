🟡 HSBC RedCoin launches today 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261023-hk-banks-news-aefdef81f0
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Payments, Digital wallets, Tokenised investments

HSBC states that its HKD-denominated RedCoin (RCHKD) became available on PayMe on 23 October 2026. It says PayMe users will be able to activate an in-app stablecoin wallet for peer-to-peer transfers and peer-to-merchant payments at more than 100,000 merchants. HSBC also says it plans later-2026 mobile-app wallet functionality, tokenised-investment subscriptions and wholesale use cases. This product announcement is not an HKMA decision or an obligation for other institutions, but it is a Hong Kong payment and merchant-acceptance benchmark; the announcement does not evidence eligibility, redemption, resilience or operating-control outcomes.

Source: HSBC RedCoin launches today

🟡 Canada Gazette: Stablecoin Regulations 🇨🇦
Item ID: item-20261024-daily-exploratory-search-ec49ce890c
⏰ Deadline: 23 December 2026 (consultation representations due within 60 days of publication)
Products: Payments, Digital assets, Cross-border services

Canada’s Department of Finance prepublished proposed Stablecoin Regulations on 24 October 2026, alongside related amendments to the Retail Payment Activities Regulations. The consultation proposes details on issuer registration, one-to-one reserves, redemption, governance, data security, recovery and resolution, reporting and notices, and would bring specified stablecoin payment functions by PSPs into the Canadian framework. Representations are invited within 60 days of publication, and the stated intention is commencement with the Act in fall 2027. This is a Canadian consultation rather than final law or a Hong Kong requirement; it may affect any Canada-facing stablecoin, wallet, custody, PSP or provider operating model, subject to the final scope and exclusions.

Source: Canada Gazette, Part I, Volume 160, Number 43: Stablecoin Regulations
