Hong Kong


🟡 Participating banks complete first IP Financing Sandbox pilot credit approvals 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260930-hkma-press-a41ebf3faf
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: SME lending; IP-backed finance

HKMA says three participating banks completed credit approvals for seven IP Financing Sandbox pilot cases, with loans of HK$1 million to HK$39 million for working capital, expansion and market promotion. The December 2025 Sandbox uses independent IP valuation reports in credit assessment and is described as a risk-controlled pilot supported by government bodies and HKAB. This is a voluntary product-development initiative, not a prescribed IP-collateral methodology, underwriting threshold or broader participation requirement; banks considering such lending may assess valuation, collateral enforceability and related credit-risk capabilities.

Source: Participating banks in IP Financing Sandbox complete credit approval for first batch of pilot cases


🟡 HSBC names planned Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin “HSBC RedCoin” 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260930-hk-banks-news-hsbc-redcoin
⏰ Deadline: Planned launch in the second half of 2026; no specific source date stated
Products: P2P payments; merchant payments; mobile app

HSBC says its forthcoming Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin will be called HSBC RedCoin and that it has not issued stablecoins in Hong Kong. It plans a second-half 2026 launch through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App, initially for P2P and P2M payments, with potential later wholesale corporate and institutional use cases. This is an official bank statement rather than an HKMA or SFC instrument, so it does not verify licence terms, a confirmed launch date, corporate availability or cross-border scope; comparable propositions may warrant preparation on redemption communications and fraud operations.

Source: HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

Material Losses, Fines & Enforcement


🟡 SFC fines Zheng Da HK$7 million over AML/CFT and control failures 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20260929-hk-enforcement-actions-ae6c90119d
⏰ Deadline: Responsible-officer suspension ends 27 April 2027; no broader source deadline stated
Products: Futures trading; securities onboarding; order systems

SFC reprimanded and fined Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited HK$7 million for AML/CFT and other failures during 1 December 2021 to 30 September 2023, and suspended responsible officer Zhong Hao from 28 September 2026 to 27 April 2027. SFC found inadequate due diligence and testing of customer-supplied futures-order systems, unsupported deposit enquiries in eight client accounts, and ineffective monitoring of suspicious trading; Zheng Da disabled the systems from August 2025. This final, case-specific disciplinary action concerns an SFC Type 2/5 intermediary and existing AML/CFT, AML Guideline and Code obligations, not a new HKMA requirement; comparable securities businesses may test system approval, surveillance and escalation evidence.

Source: SFC reprimands and fines Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited $7 million and suspends its responsible officer for regulatory breaches

🟡 FCA opens applications for regulated crypto-firm authorisation 🇬🇧
Item ID: item-20260930-fca-benchmark-de8a3eb453
⏰ Deadline: UK continuation applications: 28 February 2027; UK regime commencement: 25 October 2027
Products: Cryptoassets; digital payments; investments

FCA opened applications on 30 September 2026 for UK crypto-firm authorisation and says firms intending to continue operating in the UK should apply by 28 February 2027, ahead of the new regime taking effect on 25 October 2027. FCA says applicants must demonstrate standards on consumer protection, safeguarding customer assets, market integrity and financial resilience, while existing firms applying during the period may continue services as an undecided application is assessed. These are UK requirements rather than Hong Kong obligations; for a group entity operating in the UK, the announcement is an authorisation-readiness trigger, while for Hong Kong it is only an analogous benchmark.

Source: FCA opens the gateway to regulated crypto
Primary source: official document
