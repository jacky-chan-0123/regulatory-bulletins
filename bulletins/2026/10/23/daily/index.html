Hong Kong


🔴 Anti-bribery & corruption controls for account opening 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261022-hkma-brdr-0c7e79396a
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: SME onboarding, Digital onboarding

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) issued a current circular to all Authorized Institutions following cases in which criminal syndicates offered bribes to bypass AML/CFT controls during account opening. HKMA expects a layered, risk-based approach, including proportionate customer due-diligence information, and says inconsistencies may trigger enhanced review or account rejection. Segregation of duties must be applied during account opening, and institutions using third-party agents to introduce or assist clients must implement enhanced effective controls including due diligence, approval scrutiny and management oversight. HKMA also says institutions should consider bribery risk in ML/TF assessments, develop management information and review controls through a gap analysis; it states no fixed implementation deadline.

Actions:
• Immediately identify account-opening journeys involving branches, staff overrides and third-party agents, and confirm that segregation of duties is applied.
• In the near term, assess agent due diligence, approval scrutiny, management oversight and relevant monitoring information against the circular; escalate material gaps and retain the assessment evidence.

Source: HKMA circular — Anti-bribery & corruption controls for account opening
