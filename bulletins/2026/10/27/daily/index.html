Hong Kong


🟡 SFC approves first-ever secondary trading of tokenised public funds 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261026-sfc-web-26pr174
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Tokenised funds; virtual-asset trading; digital payments

On 26 October 2026, the Securities and Futures Commission approved secondary trading of two existing SFC-authorised tokenised money-market funds on an SFC-licensed virtual-asset trading platform, with around-the-clock trading beginning that day. The SFC states that its April 2026 pilot framework sets requirements and safeguards for trading-channel operation, fair pricing, liquidity provision and disclosure. This is the first stated live approval under that existing pilot rather than a new rule or a direct instruction to institutions outside the approved arrangement; the SFC will monitor activity and may consider broader product scope. Banks with authorised-fund, digital-asset distribution, tokenised-deposit or settlement roles may need to monitor whether a future expansion affects their product, disclosure, pricing, liquidity, settlement or 24/7 operating model.

Source: SFC approves first-ever secondary trading of tokenised public funds


🟡 HSBC launches new AI-enabled solution to transform transaction banking 🇭🇰
Item ID: item-20261026-hk-banks-news-808c29cdd6
⏰ Deadline: No source deadline stated
Products: Transaction banking; APIs; corporate treasury

On 26 October 2026, HSBC announced HSBCnio, an AI-enabled transaction-banking solution for corporate and institutional clients that supports web/mobile access through existing HSBCnet credentials and permissions, as well as API connections to client systems. HSBC says client AI tools can access authorised account and transaction data on a read-only basis through its Model Context Protocol, using established permissions and appropriate controls. Stated use cases include cash and transaction views, trade loans, FX, payment tracking and cross-border cash analysis; HSBC says the planned Ask HSBC feature is not yet current. This is a peer-bank product announcement, not a Hong Kong regulatory change, approval or sector-wide obligation, but it provides a commercial/control benchmark for permissions, customer-data access, API integration and AI/vendor governance in corporate treasury workflows.

Source: HSBC launches new AI-enabled solution to transform transaction banking
