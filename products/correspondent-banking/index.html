OCC Imposes $750 Million Assessment Against Bank of America for Deficient BSA/AML Compliance

The OCC issued a final consent order imposing a US$750 million civil money penalty on Bank of America for continuing BSA/AML-program deficiencies, providing an analogous non-binding control benchmark for Hong Kong firms.
