HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-23

Gazettal of revised SPM module CA-B-2 “Systemically Important Banks”

HKMA gazetted CA-B-2 V.3 effective 23 October 2026, replacing V.2. The revised D-SIB assessment adds RTGS/FPS turnover to deposits and customer lending as substitutability indicators; all licensed banks are in the regular assessment scope and other authorised institutions may be included case by case.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-10-19

Building the future of finance in the age of AI

In a drafted speech, the Financial Conduct Authority shared AI good and poor practice drawn from firm testing, covering customer escalation, governance, testing, monitoring, resilience and supplier dependencies. The FCA said significant AI failures and near misses should be covered by UK major-incident reporting and said it will develop an agentic-finance framework. This is a UK speech and an analogous control benchmark, not a Hong Kong obligation.

SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-14

MAS Revokes the Major Payment Institution License of Sir Money Changer Pte Ltd

MAS revoked Sir Money Changer Pte. Ltd.'s Major Payment Institution Licence effective 15 September 2026 after finding that a third party independently operated a cross-border transfer business under its name and that the firm provided false or misleading information. MAS states that licensees must not allow third parties to use their licences and payment providers must maintain effective oversight and control.

UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-09-23

Firms crack down on money mules but need to do more

The FCA reports a completed review of 35 UK retail banks, payment institutions and e-money institutions. It reports suspected mule-account closures and multi-account cash-out patterns, and says it is working with industry and law enforcement on intelligence sharing; this is a UK benchmark, not a Hong Kong requirement.

The Hong Kong Government says the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau will consult later in 2026 on expanding a legal framework to encourage technology and telecommunications sectors to improve detection and removal of potentially fraudulent online content, including AI-generated content.
