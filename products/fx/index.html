SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-14

MAS Revokes the Major Payment Institution License of Sir Money Changer Pte Ltd

MAS revoked Sir Money Changer Pte. Ltd.'s Major Payment Institution Licence effective 15 September 2026 after finding that a third party independently operated a cross-border transfer business under its name and that the firm provided false or misleading information. MAS states that licensees must not allow third parties to use their licences and payment providers must maintain effective oversight and control.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-09-16

The Chief Executive's 2026 Policy Address — International Financial Centre

The Policy Address says the HKMA will introduce a seven-day offshore RMB liquidity tender mechanism, explore offshore RMB short-term debt instruments, and encourage more foreign banks in Hong Kong to join CIPS directly. It is market-development direction, not a stated binding requirement or timetable for authorised institutions.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority, Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-08

Record year for investment product sales in Hong Kong with robust demand for FICC-related products: SFC-HKMA joint survey 2025

The HKMA and SFC reported that 2025 non-exchange-traded investment-product sales reached HK$9.9 trillion, up 63% year on year; online sales were 21% of aggregate transaction value and currency-linked product sales were HK$698 billion. The release reports survey results and states no new deadline or requirement.
