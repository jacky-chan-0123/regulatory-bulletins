Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) amendments

Hong Kong subsidiary legislation scheduled to commence on 1 March 2027 permits continuing and post-listing conditions and issuer information requisitions. It is required for parties within its statutory perimeter; the source does not establish a direct bank-wide obligation.
