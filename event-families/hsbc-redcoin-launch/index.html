HSBC RedCoin launches today

HSBC states that its HKD-denominated RedCoin became available on PayMe on 23 October 2026. It says users will be able to activate wallets for peer-to-peer and merchant payments, with later-2026 plans for mobile-app wallets, tokenised-investment subscriptions and wholesale use cases.
