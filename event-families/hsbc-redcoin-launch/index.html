HSBC RedCoin launches today

HSBC says its HKD stablecoin, RedCoin (RCHKD), will be available from launch on PayMe. In the coming days, the bank says more than 3.3 million PayMe users will be able to activate an in-app wallet and use it for peer-to-peer and peer-to-merchant payments, including at more than 100,000 merchants; it also describes later planned mobile-app, tokenised-investment and wholesale use cases.
