HK · Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited · 2026-10-16

Implementation of T+1 Settlement Cycle for Hong Kong Cash Market

HKSCC states that, subject to market readiness and regulatory approval, Hong Kong cash-market settlement will move from T+2 to T+1 in the second half of 2028. The circular asks Clearing Participants to assess T-day post-trade operations, liquidity and funding, client cut-offs and communications, customer documents, and exception controls.

HK · Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region · 2026-10-07

LCQ17: Accelerated settlement for cash market

The Hong Kong Government says HKEX is preparing a proposed T+1 cash-market settlement cycle and targets consultation conclusions and implementation arrangements within 2026; no final implementation date or participant requirements are stated.
