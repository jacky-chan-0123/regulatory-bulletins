Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-22

GS-2 Transition Planning — corrected finding

HKMA issued GS-2 V.1 on 22 September 2026 as a non-statutory SPM guidance note applicable to all authorized institutions. It sets supervisory expectations for governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets, stakeholder engagement, scenario analysis and communication. AIs should have transition-planning documentation in place by 1 July 2030, take active implementation steps beforehand, and be prepared to demonstrate progress 24 months after issuance.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-23

Review of the Enhanced Competency Framework for Banking Practitioners

HKMA proposes a revised Enhanced Competency Framework for all AIs, including a common Core Level, ten Professional-Level workstreams, revised experience requirements and integrated qualifications. It advises uninterrupted current ECF training while guidance and transition arrangements are developed; milestones are indicative rather than a fixed compliance date.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-09

Circular on Private Market Investments

HKMA reminded registered institutions distributing publicly or privately offered products with private-credit or private-equity exposure to apply product due diligence, appropriate risk ratings, clear risk disclosure, suitability controls, monitoring and staff training. The circular states no source deadline and preserves existing institutional and qualified-corporate professional-investor exemptions.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-07

Early Alert on United Nations Sanctions

The SFC expects licensed corporations, SFC-licensed virtual-asset service providers and associated entities to update customer- and payment-screening databases for two UN sanctions-list amendments. It states no deadline.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-03

Remote on-boarding of individual customers

HKMA updated supervisory expectations for remote onboarding of individual customers by authorised institutions and stored value facility licensees. The circular expects ongoing use of fraud and scam intelligence, continual solution updates and recalibration, reliable identity-document authentication, identity matching, and a comprehensive effectiveness review.

Employers' data in loan applications: PCPD findings
