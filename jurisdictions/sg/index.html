SG · Monetary Authority of Singapore · 2026-10-14

MAS Revokes the Major Payment Institution License of Sir Money Changer Pte Ltd

MAS revoked Sir Money Changer Pte. Ltd.'s Major Payment Institution Licence effective 15 September 2026 after finding that a third party independently operated a cross-border transfer business under its name and that the firm provided false or misleading information. MAS states that licensees must not allow third parties to use their licences and payment providers must maintain effective oversight and control.

Singapore's Ministry of Home Affairs described proposed scam-countermeasure orders and said local banks will offer an LPBA or alternative basic account from October 2026, with no exact date stated.

SG · DBS, OCBC, UOB · 2026-09-10

DBS, OCBC and UOB complete first live tokenised-deposit transactions on Swift’s ledger

DBS, OCBC and UOB announced that they completed live domestic Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits on Swift’s blockchain-based ledger. The banks say the work provides insights for round-the-clock interbank payments and may support future corporate treasury, trade and digital-commerce services; it is not a Hong Kong regulatory requirement or a confirmed customer rollout.
