HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-11-10

Northern Metropolis Financial Advisory Taskforce holds its third meeting

HKMA proposes amending the Banking (Capital) Rules to designate specified government-related entities as Domestic Public Sector Entities to support financing for Northern Metropolis projects. The proposal is not enacted; HKMA intends to submit amendments to Legislative Council in Q1 2027, with a view to effect in Q2 2027.
