HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-12

SFC approves six securities registrars for upcoming uncertificated securities market regime

The SFC approved six securities registrars effective 16 November 2026, when the USM regime takes effect. Listed issuers of prescribed securities must maintain an approved registrar; the stated requirements apply to issuers and registrars, not every bank.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-09

Project Liquidity: Engineering the Market Plumbing that Unlocks Capital Flows for Hong Kong

The SFC published a speech describing implemented and prospective HKEX clearing, collateral and settlement initiatives, including a March 2027 client-margin phase-2 target subject to regulatory approval. It is an official market-development signal, not a binding direction; final HKEX approvals, scope and terms remain to be published.
