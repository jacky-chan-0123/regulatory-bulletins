HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-28

Supplemental circular to management companies of SFC-authorised funds on liquidity risk management

The SFC circular sets enhanced liquidity-risk-management guidance for managers of SFC-authorised open-ended funds, including expectations on liquidity assessment, liquidity-management tools, investor disclosure and reporting when specified tools or measures are activated.

Hong Kong · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-10-26

SFC approves first-ever secondary trading of tokenised public funds

The SFC approved secondary trading of two existing SFC-authorised tokenised money-market funds on an SFC-licensed virtual-asset trading platform, with around-the-clock trading beginning on 26 October 2026. The approval applies the April 2026 pilot framework; it is not a new sector-wide rule or direct instruction outside the approved arrangement.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission, Accounting and Financial Reporting Council, The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited · 2026-10-08

SFC, AFRC and the Exchange join forces to address disclaimers of opinion solely relating to going concern

The SFC, AFRC and the Exchange issued a joint statement setting supervisory expectations for listed issuers with audit disclaimers solely relating to going concern. It expects robust assessments supported by reasonable assumptions, reliable data and feasible action plans, alongside detailed annual and quarterly progress disclosures and critical audit-committee and auditor review. The statement does not amend Listing Rules or set a new deadline; the Exchange may consider rule changes if improvement is not significant.
