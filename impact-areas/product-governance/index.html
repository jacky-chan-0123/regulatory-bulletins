UK · Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-09-16

Crypto firms get guidance on how the new regime applies

The FCA says its future UK cryptoasset-regime guidance explains which activities may require authorisation, including qualifying-stablecoin issuance, platform operation, dealing or arranging, safeguarding and arranging staking. Applications open on 30 September 2026 and the regime is due to enter force on 25 October 2027; this is a UK benchmark, not a Hong Kong requirement.

Hong Kong · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-09-09

Circular on Private Market Investments

HKMA reminded registered institutions distributing publicly or privately offered products with private-credit or private-equity exposure to apply product due diligence, appropriate risk ratings, clear risk disclosure, suitability controls, monitoring and staff training. The circular states no source deadline and preserves existing institutional and qualified-corporate professional-investor exemptions.

HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority, Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-08

Record year for investment product sales in Hong Kong with robust demand for FICC-related products: SFC-HKMA joint survey 2025

The HKMA and SFC reported that 2025 non-exchange-traded investment-product sales reached HK$9.9 trillion, up 63% year on year; online sales were 21% of aggregate transaction value and currency-linked product sales were HK$698 billion. The release reports survey results and states no new deadline or requirement.

HK · Securities and Futures Commission · 2026-09-03

Circular on SFC-authorised funds with exposure to private market assets

The SFC requires enhanced offering-document disclosures for affected public funds with direct or indirect exposure to private market assets. Funds with aggregate direct and indirect exposure of 50% or more of NAV will be considered complex products, and existing fund managers are expected to review funds, update documents as soon as practicable, and communicate properly with distributors.
