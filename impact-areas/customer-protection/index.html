HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-10-15

Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence

HKMA issued updated ART supervisory guidance for all Authorized Institutions. Annex A supersedes the 2019 AI principles and expects proportionate governance across the AI lifecycle, including ownership, inventory, risk-based testing and monitoring, recovery, records, data and third-party safeguards, and fair and transparent customer use. HKMA states that policies, procedures and controls should be uplifted in a timely manner; no fixed deadline is stated. Annex B provides non-exhaustive Sandbox insights, while Annex C is preliminary and does not establish current agentic-AI requirements.

HK · HSBC · 2026-09-30

HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

HSBC announced the name of its planned Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin and says it plans a phased H2 2026 launch, initially through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App for P2P and P2M payments. The bank statement does not confirm a launch date, licence terms, corporate availability, redemption design or cross-border scope.

SG · Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs · 2026-09-09

Second Reading of the Scams (Countermeasures) and Other Matters Bill – Wrap-Up Speech

Singapore's Ministry of Home Affairs described proposed scam-countermeasure orders and said local banks will offer an LPBA or alternative basic account from October 2026, with no exact date stated.
