HSBC names stablecoin ‘HSBC Redcoin’

HSBC announced the name of its planned Hong Kong-dollar stablecoin and says it plans a phased H2 2026 launch, initially through PayMe and the HSBC HK Mobile App for P2P and P2M payments.
