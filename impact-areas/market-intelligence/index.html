Record year for investment product sales in Hong Kong with robust demand for FICC-related products: SFC-HKMA joint survey 2025

The HKMA and SFC reported that 2025 non-exchange-traded investment-product sales reached HK$9.9 trillion, up 63% year on year; online sales were 21% of aggregate transaction value and currency-linked product sales were HK$698 billion. The release reports survey results and states no new deadline or requirement.
