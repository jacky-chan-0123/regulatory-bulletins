HK · Hong Kong Monetary Authority · 2026-11-19

Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks

HKMA expects all authorised institutions to review the HKAB Guideline on Corruption Prevention for Banks, assess current policies and procedures, plan necessary enhancements where appropriate, and report compliance status by end-Q3 2027. The Guideline consolidates existing requirements and practical anti-corruption measures; the circular is a supervisory expectation, not a new statutory rule or prescribed uniform control design.

Guideline on Corruption Prevention issued by Hong Kong Association of Banks

HKMA's circular to all authorized institutions introduces HKAB's guideline. HKMA expects assessment against its good practices, enhancements where appropriate, and a compliance-status report by end-Q3 2027. The circular describes the guideline as a consolidated reference that reinforces existing regulations and statutory requirements.
